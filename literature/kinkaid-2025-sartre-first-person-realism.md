# James Kinkaid (2025) — *A ‘Drainage Hole’ in Being: Sartre and First-Person Realism*

> 阅读状态：**publisher fulltext-read (Cambridge HTML)**。已直接核对 §1–§5，重点读取 §4.1 man-in-the-park、§4.2 voyeur / standpoint 与结论。
>
> James Kinkaid, “A ‘Drainage Hole’ in Being: Sartre and First-Person Realism”, *Journal of the American Philosophical Association* 11(4), 2025, 782–799. Published online 5 May 2025. Open access.

## 1. 核心用途

Kinkaid 把 Sartre 与 contemporary first-person realism 对接，重点攻击 **inegalitarian** first-person realism，尤其 Merlo 的 metaphysical-solipsism argument。

Merlo 的结构：

\[
PhenomenologicalSolipsism
+
AppearanceRealityPrinciple
\Rightarrow
MetaphysicalSolipsism.
\]

具体：

\[
\text{it appears that only these/my experiences have feltness}
\]

再加 appearance/reality bridge，推出：

\[
\text{only these/my experiences have feltness in reality}.
\]

Kinkaid 用 Sartre 的 encounter-with-the-other phenomenology攻击第一前提。

---

## 2. Man in the park：other appears as another center

Sartre 的关键 phenomenology是：遇到另一个人时，对方不是普通 object inside my egocentric field。

他被 experience 成：

\[
\boxed{\text{another center of orientation / another ipseity}.}
\]

该 other 打开自己的 hodological space：objects 具有相对于其 goals / possibilities 的 directions / distances / valences。

Kinkaid 强调：other appears as a zero-point of orientation，拥有自己的 “world” / mineness。

所以：

\[
\boxed{
\text{my experience of others itself contains evidence of plural centers}.}
\]

这与 Merlo：

\[
\text{phenomenologically only my experiences have feltness}
\]

直接冲突。

---

## 3. Phenomenological disagreement 不是逻辑 refutation

Kinkaid 的 strongest claim 是 phenomenological plausibility：他认为 Sartre 的 description比 Merlo 更可信。

但这并不形成形式 theorem。

理论上 Merlo 可以说：

- other appears *as if* it has a center；
- the represented other-center不等于 genuine feltness simpliciter；
- current subject仍唯一实际 possess feltness。

所以 Kinkaid最稳作用是：

\[
\boxed{
\text{inegalitarian phenomenology no longer has uncontested datum status}.}
\]

absolute positive side不能把：

> “别人显然只像对象，我这里显然唯一 live”

当作 neutral phenomenological starting point。

---

## 4. Voyeur：other-as-subject can be a metaphysical standpoint

Kinkaid用 Lipman 2023 的 standpoint concept解释 Sartre’s other-as-subject：

\[
Standpoint(x)\approx \text{something relative to which genuine facts obtain}.
\]

在 “the look” / shame 中，other standpoint confers an outside / social self on me。

这不是 mere epistemic relation：某些 facts about my social self因 other standpoint而 obtain。

因此 Sartre-style intersubjectivity可以被 formalized 为：

\[
\boxed{\text{plural world-side standpoints}.}
\]

而不需要 one globally privileged standpoint。

这进一步加强 Lipman/Eker 型 pluralist competitor。

---

## 5. Natural solipsism 的 limit

Kinkaid并不否认 Sartre 对 local ipseity 的强刻画。

subject的 world initially / locally围绕 own possibilities organization；这很接近：

\[
LocalCenter(S).
\]

但 experience of the other给 this “natural solipsism” 加 limit：

\[
LocalMineness(S_1)
\]

与：

\[
RecognizedOtherMineness(S_2)
\]

可以同时属于 phenomenology。

所以 local centeredness的 phenomenological strength并不产生 exclusivity。

---

## 6. Detotalized totality / fragmentalism

Kinkaid 最后指出 Sartre 的 “detotalized totality” 与 Fine/Lipman fragmentalist picture有亲缘性：reality不必形成一个单一 globally coherent perspective-stack。

这对项目意味着：

\[
\boxed{
\text{phenomenological realism about multiple centers can motivate plural/fragment architecture rather than one absolute center}.}
\]

因此 phenomenology本身不偏向 inegalitarian conclusion。

---

## 7. 对 direct-absolute seeming 的影响

Hare / Merlo 风格正方会诉诸：

\[
\text{manifest / immediate appearance that here is uniquely present/felt}.
\]

Kinkaid 提供一个同样 phenomenological 的 counterpressure：

\[
\text{others appear as genuine other centers}. 
\]

这使证据状态变成：

\[
\boxed{\text{phenomenology contains both local self-centrality and encountered alterity}.}
\]

从两者中直接推出 one global absolute center需要新的 inference；不能只称前者“更直接”就结束。
