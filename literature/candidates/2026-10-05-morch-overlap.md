## 1. Core claim

Mørch把 overlapping consciousness 定义为：一个 unified conscious system 含有自身也 unified-conscious 的 proper parts / overlapping subsystems。

其 manuscript 摘要声称：

- 在 deflationary bundle view of subjects 下，overlap会导向 straightforward contradiction；
- 在 substantive subject view 下，overlap原则上可行，但高度 unparsimonious；
- 因而任何理论若 imply overlap，应拒绝或修改 overlap。

## 2. Why this matters now

### Against Subject Universalism

Osborn universalism让任意 subject fusion也是 subject，因而大量 subjects彼此 overlap。

若 Mørch 的 anti-overlap argument成功：

\[
SubjectUniversalism\to Overlap\to Contradiction/SevereCost.
\]

这会直接攻击 composition-first Global Closure route。

### Against Continuism

Roelofs continuism允许不同 ordinary human consciousnesses phenomenally co-conscious / share a larger field，同时保持 informational boundaries。

这通常需要某种 overlapping / shared experiential ownership architecture。

若 overlap impossible：

\[
Continuism\text{ faces direct pressure}.
\]

## 3. Important caution

当前只有摘要级证据，不能确认：

- “contradiction” 的精确 premises；
- bundle-view argument 是否依赖 exclusivity of experience ownership；
- 是否允许 one field with informational subregions但拒绝 subject overlap；
- 对 Roelofs 的 continuism到底是直接反例还是仅要求重述 subject ontology。

因此暂不升级成 literature/index 的 decisive objection。

## 4. Next full-text audit questions

1. Overlap contradiction 是否来自 `subject = bundle of experiences` + exclusive ownership？

4. continuism 能否保留 one field同时把 local humans视为 informational agents而非 phenomenally bounded subjects？
5. subject universalism 能否用 substantive subject theory承受 parsimony cost，而避免 contradiction？
