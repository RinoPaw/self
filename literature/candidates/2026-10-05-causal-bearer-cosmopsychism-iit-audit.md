# Causal / Global Bearer Audit — Cosmopsychism, Animalism, IIT

> Date: 2026-10-05
>
> Status: candidate literature audit.
>
> Target:
>
> \[
> \boxed{Can\ causal/system\ unity\ independently\ determine\ one\ subject\ of\ experience?}
> \]

## 1. Executive result

No reviewed route gives a neutral theorem from

\[
OneCausalOrFunctionalWhole
\]

to

\[
OneSubjectOfExperience.
\]

The strongest available bridges are theory-laden:

- **animalism** identifies the subject with the organism and can therefore preserve one subject through severe functional/phenomenal disunity;
- **IIT** derives a maximal substrate from phenomenological axioms and then identifies the maximally irreducible cause–effect structure with one experience;
- **cosmopsychism** begins from a conscious cosmic whole and then faces a de-combination / individuation problem in deriving local subjects.

Thus causal or mereological unity is not by itself a discriminator of experiential subjecthood.

## 2. Animalism: a genuine countermodel to PFIP, but not a neutral bridge

### Sources

- Paul F. Snowdon, “Animalism and the Unity of Consciousness” (2014; reprinted 2016).
- SEP, “Animalism”, substantive revision 2026.

Snowdon explicitly argues that animalism is committed to a **singularist** reading of split-brain cases: the post-operative mental states can remain states of one subject despite functional disunity.

This is an important live countermodel to unconditional LUP/PFIP:

\[
SameOrganism
+ FunctionalDisunity
\not\Rightarrow
MultipleSubjects.
\]

But the explanatory direction is crucial. Animalism does not infer subject-unity from independently established phenomenal or causal unity. It starts with the metaphysical identity claim:

\[
Subject = Animal
\]

and then interprets the divided mental states as belonging to that one animal-subject.

So animalism establishes **coherence of extra-phenomenal bearer identity**, not a general bridge from causal unity to subjecthood.

## 3. Cosmopsychism: global whole first, local subjecthood remains a problem

### Sources

- Itay Shani, “Cosmopsychism: A Holistic Approach to the Metaphysics of Experience” (2015).
- Gregory Miller, “Can Subjects Be Proper Parts of Subjects? The De-Combination Problem” (2017/2018).
- Shani & Keppler, “Beyond Combination: How Cosmic Consciousness Grounds Ordinary Experience” (2018).
- SEP Panpsychism supplement, Spring 2026.

Cosmopsychism combines priority-monist structure with a conscious cosmic ultimate. But priority monism by itself gives only:

\[
OneFundamentalConcreteWhole.
\]

The further claim

\[
OneFundamentalConcreteWhole
\Rightarrow
OneCosmicSubject
\]

requires a psychophysical / phenomenal premise.

Moreover, once a cosmic subject is posited, the theory inherits the **de-combination problem**: in virtue of what does one cosmic subject ground local human and animal subjects?

Miller argues that the problem mirrors the subject-combination problem because both concern subject-subject proper-parthood relations and phenomenal unity/boundedness.

The current SEP summary likewise treats constitutive cosmopsychism as facing an analogous explanatory gap: a conscious universe can be conceived without conscious local parts, so local subjects do not follow trivially from the cosmic subject.

Thus cosmopsychism does not supply the required neutral BDP bridge. It presupposes cosmic subjecthood and then owes a further individuation relation for local subjects.

## 4. IIT is the strongest explicit causal-structural discriminator

### Source

Albantakis et al., “Integrated Information Theory (IIT) 4.0” (2023).

IIT provides a serious candidate mechanism for definite subject/experience boundaries. It starts from phenomenological axioms including:

- intrinsicality;
- integration;
- exclusion / definiteness.

These are translated into physical postulates. Candidate substrates are compared by system integrated information; a maximal substrate or **complex** is selected, and overlapping lower candidates are excluded.

Schematically:

\[
PhenomenalDefiniteness
+ IntegrationAxiom
+ MaximalExistencePrinciple
\Rightarrow
MaximalIrreducibleSubstrate.
\]

This is much stronger than generic causal connectedness. It gives a concrete discriminator over overlapping systems.

However, the bridge is not purely causal. IIT explicitly infers its physical postulates from phenomenological axioms and then identifies the resulting maximally irreducible structure with experience. The key bridge therefore has roughly the form:

\[
PhenomenologicalAxioms
\Rightarrow
PhysicalPostulates
\Rightarrow
Complex
\equiv
Experience.
\]

So IIT does not show:

\[
CausalIntegrationAlone\Rightarrow Subjecthood.
\]

It shows that **given IIT's phenomenology-to-physics identity framework**, maximal intrinsic causal power can individuate the substrate of one experience.

## 5. IIT is structurally localizing, not naturally Universal-I-friendly

A particularly important result for this project is IIT's recursive exclusion procedure. Within a universal substrate, one maximal complex is identified, its units are excluded, then the remaining substrate is searched for further complexes.

That architecture tends toward a plurality of disjoint maximal complexes rather than one cosmic complex merely because the universe is causally connected.

Therefore generic appeals such as:

\[
UniverseIsOneCausalNetwork
\Rightarrow
UniverseIsOneSubject
\]

receive no support from IIT. A cosmic subject would require the **universe itself** to be the relevant maximal irreducible complex under IIT's metric.

This is an empirical/theory-specific condition, not a consequence of universal causal connection.

## 6. Why IIT cannot simply settle BDP

Two limitations matter.

First, IIT's crucial inference from the phenomenology of definiteness/integration to a maximal causal substrate is theory-internal and contested. Critics have specifically targeted the exclusion step and the move from phenomenological unity to physical maximality.

Second, even granting IIT, what is directly individuated is a maximal substrate corresponding to an experience/Φ-structure. A further philosophical question remains about whether “subject” should be identified with each such experiential complex, with a temporally extended sequence of complexes, or with something else.

So IIT is the strongest constructive model of a **Bearer Discriminator**, but not a theory-neutral proof of one.

## 8. Current verdict

The causal/global bearer horn narrows to:

\[
\boxed{
ExtraPhenomenalBearer
\Rightarrow
IndependentIndividuationTheory
}
\]

and the reviewed theories divide as follows:

- animalism supplies a bearer criterion but pays by making subject count follow organism count even under phenomenal disunity;
- IIT supplies a nontrivial structural criterion, but only through a phenomenology-loaded identity framework;
- cosmopsychism does not derive cosmic subjecthood from mere causal unity and still faces the local de-combination problem.

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{
GlobalCausalUnity\not\Rightarrow OneExperientialSubject.
}
\]

The Universal-I cannot be rescued merely by saying the universe is one causally connected or mereologically unified whole.

## 9. Next pressure point

The strongest remaining Universal-I options are now:

1. **IIT-like global maximality:** show the universe is literally one maximally irreducible experiential substrate;
2. **primitive cosmic subject:** take cosmic bearer identity as fundamental;
3. **special psychophysical law:** independently assign all experiential fields to one bearer.

(1) is substantive and testable inside a theory but receives no generic support from causal connectedness. (2) and (3) move back toward the primitive-bearer / assignment-principle horn already isolated by BDP.
