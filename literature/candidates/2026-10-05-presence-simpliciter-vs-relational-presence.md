# Presence Simpliciter vs Relational Presence — CC1 Audit

> Date: 2026-10-05
>
> Status: candidate literature audit.
>
> Target question:
>
> \[
> \boxed{Does\ ordinary\ temporal/experiential\ presence\ force\ presence\ simpliciter?}
> \]

## 1. Executive result

Current literature does **not** establish:

\[
PhenomenalNow + ExperientialImmediacy
\Rightarrow
PresenceSimpliciter.
\]

The strongest positive route is conditional:

\[
ATheory(PresenceSimpliciter)
\Rightarrow
PressureTowardPersonalizedPresence.
\]

Conitzer argues that A-theorists who metaphysically privilege the Now should also take seriously a metaphysically privileged I. His argument therefore starts from a realist A-theoretic premise; it does not derive that premise from neutral phenomenology alone.

Hare's perspectival realism is itself evidence that the phenomenological data can be given a subject-relative realist reading without first privileging a global temporal Now.

Recent eternalist / B-theoretic work also offers a direct rival explanation of the felt present in terms of temporally extended awareness, memory, anticipation, and self-modeling.

Therefore `PresenceSimpliciter` currently fails the project’s CC1 test as an **independent completion witness**.

## 2. Conitzer: conditional internal pressure, not a phenomenology-to-metaphysics proof

### Source

Vincent Conitzer, “The Personalized A-Theory of Time and Perspective,” *Dialectica* 74(1), 2020, 3–31.

Evidence level: full paper available from author site; targeted reading plus abstract/metadata verification.

### Core result

Conitzer’s explicit project is:

\[
ATheory\Rightarrow PersonalizedATheoryPressure.
\]

He compares theories where the Now is metaphysically distinguished with analogous theories where the I is metaphysically distinguished.

The paper’s headline claim is not:

\[
ExperienceAsPresent\Rightarrow ObjectivePresentness.
\]

It is closer to:

\[
If\ objective\ presentness\ is\ accepted,
\ then\ analogous\ reasons\ support\ objective\ first\!\text{-}person\ privilege.
\]

This is highly relevant to the project, but it means Conitzer cannot by himself supply CC1. He strengthens the **second half** of the bridge:

\[
PresenceSimpliciter\to PersonalizedPresence,
\]

while leaving the first half contested:

\[
OrdinaryPresencePhenomenology\to PresenceSimpliciter\;?
\]

## 3. Hare: perspectival realism weakens any forced move to temporal simpliciter

### Sources

- Caspar Hare, “Self-Bias, Time-Bias, and the Metaphysics of Self and Time,” *Journal of Philosophy* 104(7), 2007.
- Caspar Hare, *On Myself, and Other, Less Important Subjects* (Princeton UP, 2009).
- Caspar Hare, “Realism About Tense and Perspective,” *Philosophy Compass* 5(9), 2010, 760–769.

Evidence level: abstracts, publisher metadata, and existing project notes; no complete reread in this pass.

Hare explicitly treats perspectival realism as a serious neighbor of tense realism and argues that perspectival realism can retain principal virtues while avoiding principal vices of tense realism.

For the present audit, that matters because it blocks the simple inference:

\[
PhenomenalSpecialnessOfNow
\Rightarrow
GlobalTemporalPresentness.
\]

A realist about perspective can instead say that what is privileged is structurally tied to a standpoint.

This does not automatically vindicate Neutral Actuality, because Hare’s own egocentric presentism is highly asymmetric and can become solipsistic. But it demonstrates that the data underdetermine the metaphysical locus of privilege.

## 4. List’s reconstruction exposes the cost of Hare-style simpliciter presence

Christian List’s 2025 quadrilemma review characterizes Hare’s egocentric presentism as a theory on which one subject’s experiences have monadic presentness while others do not in the same way.

This clarifies an important point for the project:

\[
PresenceSimpliciter
\]

is not a small semantic gloss on ordinary for-me-ness. Once made metaphysically robust, it carries substantial world-structural commitments about which experiences are genuinely present.

That strengthens the burden of proof for treating it as an independently given phenomenological datum.

## 5. Neutral / relational rival: extended awareness and self-model explanation

Recent eternalist defenses explicitly argue that we need not experience events as metaphysically present. Instead, events are given within a temporally extended awareness window; the apparent specialness of the present is then generated through contrasts with memory and anticipation and through a self-model located in spacetime.

This provides a coherent explanatory schema:

\[
TemporalWindow
+ Memory
+ Anticipation
+ SelfModel
\Rightarrow
NowSeeming.
\]

Therefore the neutralist can accept:

- vivid current experience;
- phenomenal immediacy;
- a lived temporal center;
- de se currentness judgments;

without adding:

\[
PresentSimpliciter(E).
\]

This is enough to block a deductive CC1 witness unless the centered side can isolate a phenomenological residue not captured by the relational package.

## 6. The key distinction: presence-to vs presence-simpliciter

The project should now explicitly distinguish:

### RP — Relational Presence

\[
PresentTo(E,S,t)
\]

or more generally:

\[
ExperientiallyAvailable(E,S,t).
\]

This can be supported by ordinary phenomenology and cognitive/phenomenological structure.

### PS — Presence Simpliciter

\[
PresentSimpliciter(E).
\]

This says the event has a non-relative presentness over and above relational availability.

The desired bridge is:

\[
RP\Rightarrow PS.
\]

No source located in this pass establishes that entailment.

## 7. Why introspective certainty does not immediately help

A subject may be certain:

\[
\text{this experience is occurring now for me}.
\]

But that certainty is compatible with:

\[
PresentTo(E,S,t)
\]

without deciding whether:

\[
PresentSimpliciter(E).
\]

Thus the familiar access/ontology gap reappears:

\[
CertaintyOfCurrentExperience
\not\Rightarrow
ObjectivePresentness.
\]

The positive side needs an argument that relational presence fails to capture the content of the certainty, not merely the observation that current experience feels immediate.

## 8. CC1 verdict

For CC1 the witness must satisfy:

\[
Independent(X)
\land
NeutralActuality\not\models X
\land
CenteredActuality\models X.
\]

`PresenceSimpliciter` currently fails the **Independent(X)** requirement because its identification already imports the contested metaphysical reading of presence.

What is independently given is closer to:

\[
ExperientialImmediacy
+
PhenomenalNow
+
ForMeNess.
\]

Neutral relational models can represent those.

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{PresenceSimpliciter\text{ is currently a metaphysical interpretation of the witness, not the witness itself}.}
\]

## 9. What would reverse this verdict

A successful future argument would need one of the following:

### P1 — Phenomenal residue

Show a feature \(X\) of temporal experience such that:

\[
RelationalPresence\not\models X.
\]

### P2 — Semantic indispensability

Show that the content of genuine present-tense or first-person temporal judgment cannot be truth-conditionally captured without monadic presentness.

### P3 — Explanatory asymmetry

Show that relational accounts only reproduce behavior/representation while failing to explain a first-personally manifest feature that centered presence explains without restating it.

### P4 — Incoherence of pure relational presence

Derive a contradiction or regress from:

\[
AllPresenceIsRelational.
\]

No such result is established in this pass.

## 10. Current conclusion

\[
\boxed{
Ordinary\ presence\ phenomenology
\not\Rightarrow
PresenceSimpliciter
}
\]

as a current research verdict.

But a narrower positive conditional survives:

\[
\boxed{
PresenceSimpliciter
\Rightarrow
Personalized\ I\text{-}NOW\ pressure
}
\]

via Conitzer/Hare-style reasoning.

So the bottleneck is now sharply identified:

\[
\boxed{
RelationalPresence\stackrel{?}{\Rightarrow}PresenceSimpliciter.
}
\]

That, rather than generalized A-theory, is the remaining high-value target.
