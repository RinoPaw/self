# Global Subject Maximality Audit

> Date: 2026-10-05
>
> Status: candidate literature audit.
>
> Target:
>
> \[
> \boxed{Can\ a\ subject\!\text{-}\!sensitive\ maximality\ rule\ make\ the\ cosmos\ one\ subject?}
> \]

## 1. Executive result

The audit did not find a current theory-neutral route on which the cosmos wins subjecthood merely because it is the largest or most encompassing system.

The strongest explicit maximality machinery found is IIT. But IIT's exclusion rule compares overlapping candidate substrates by **maximal intrinsic irreducible cause–effect power**, not by spatial size, causal reach, or mereological inclusiveness. Over a universal substrate it recursively selects maxima and then searches the remaining units for further complexes.

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{
LargestWhole\not\Rightarrow MaximalSubject.
}
\]

and, within IIT-style architecture:

\[
\boxed{
CosmicSubject\Rightarrow CosmosMustWinIntrinsicMaximality.
}
\]

That is a substantive extra condition.

## 2. IIT 4.0: maximality is local competition, not cosmic default

Albantakis et al. 2023 define a complex as a candidate substrate whose system integrated information exceeds that of all overlapping candidates. Exclusion gives definite borders by selecting the maximally irreducible candidate.

Crucially, the search occurs inside a universal substrate by recursion:

1. identify the winning complex;
2. exclude its units;
3. search the remainder for the next complex;
4. repeat.

This architecture naturally permits many non-overlapping complexes.

Hence IIT gives no rule:

\[
Universe\supset Everything\Rightarrow UniverseComplex.
\]

The whole universe would count as one complex only if its intrinsic irreducibility beats every overlapping candidate at the relevant grain.

## 3. 2026 IIT grain work strengthens the discriminator

Recent IIT work on intrinsic units emphasizes that a complex must beat overlapping systems **including candidates at different grains**. The point of the maximality rule is precisely to explain why a conscious substrate has the borders and constituents it does.

This sharpens BDP: a legitimate global bearer theory needs a criterion that survives comparison against local alternatives, not a rule that simply privileges the most inclusive object.

## 4. Cosmopsychist field models do not provide independent cosmic maximality

Keppler/Shani-style cosmopsychism posits a ubiquitous field of consciousness (UFC) and treats local conscious states as arising when coherent neural assemblies interact with/modulate this field.

This gives a proposed physical mechanism for local phenomenal states, but the model starts from the claim that the universal field is inherently sentient. It therefore does not independently derive:

\[
UniversalField\Rightarrow OneNumericalSubject.
\]

A field can be globally ubiquitous without its entire extent constituting one phenomenally unified subject. The theory needs an additional individuation claim if “one field” is to entail “one I”.

## 5. Generic physical field theories also do not supply the bridge

Field theories of consciousness often identify individual conscious experiences with spatially extended field configurations. This can help explain why consciousness is spatially extended and integrated inside a local system.

But physical field continuity is itself too permissive for subject individuation. Electromagnetic, gravitational, and quantum fields can extend globally while supporting many localized structures.

Thus:

\[
OnePhysicalField\not\Rightarrow OneSubject.
\]

A field theory still needs a unity/boundary/maximality criterion.

## 6. Cosmopsychism explicitly assumes more than maximal mereology

Canonical cosmopsychism is usually formulated by adding consciousness to priority monism: the cosmos is not merely the fundamental concrete whole, but a conscious cosmic subject whose properties ground local subjects.

The literature therefore confirms the direction:

\[
PriorityMonism + CosmicConsciousnessPremise\Rightarrow Cosmopsychism,
\]

not:

\[
PriorityMonism\Rightarrow CosmicConsciousness.
\]

This matters because Universal-I cannot use mereological fundamentality as its missing BDP discriminator without adding the very subject-sensitive premise under dispute.

## 7. Global subject pressure is still weaker than global subject individuation

Recent work on “global subject pressure” in idealist settings argues that certain holistic commitments create pressure toward a globally unifying subject. Even there, the result is explicitly compatible with several realizations: primitive phenomenal field, neutral-monist experiencer, cosmopsychist field consciousness, or other global unifiers.

So global explanatory pressure does not uniquely determine:

- one numerical subject;
- one phenomenal field;
- one owner relation;
- one subject boundary criterion.

It is therefore not yet the required maximality theorem.

## 8. Strongest non-primitive Universal-I candidate

The strongest surviving candidate has the following form:

### GSM — Global Subject Maximality

\[
\boxed{
There\ exists\ a\ subject\!\text{-}\!sensitive\ measure\ M
\text{ such that }
M(Cosmos)>M(S)
\text{ for every competing }S.
}
\]

plus an identity bridge:

\[
Max_M(S)\Rightarrow OneSubject(S).
\]

IIT provides the clearest model of this architecture, but does not currently provide evidence that the cosmos is the maximizer.

Field cosmopsychism provides a cosmic phenomenal base, but does not currently provide an independent numerical-subject maximality rule.

## 9. Current verdict

No reviewed source establishes:

\[
\boxed{
Cosmos\ is\ the\ winning\ subject\ under\ a\ non\!\text{-}\!question\!\text{-}\!begging\ maximality\ rule.
}
\]

The Universal-I's strongest non-primitive escape therefore remains a **research programme**, not a supplied bridge.

This leaves the locality fork as:

\[
\boxed{
GlobalMaximalityBridge\quad|\quad PrimitiveCosmicBearer.
}
\]

The first now has a clear burden of proof; the second inherits BDP's assignment problem.
