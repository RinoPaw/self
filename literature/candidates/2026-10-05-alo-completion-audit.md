# ALO Completion Audit — Presence, Opening, Self-Manifestation, and Law

> Date: 2026-10-05
>
> Status: candidate literature audit under `literature/AGENTS.md`.
>
> Target:
>
> \[
> \boxed{ALO:\quad CompleteActuality\Rightarrow\exists\sigma\;IrreducibleOpening(\sigma)}
> \]
>
> This audit is calibrated to the repository's latest actuality endgame: Neutral Actuality vs Constitutive Role-First Centered Actuality. A route counts as progress only if it bears on CC1 (independent completion witness), CC2 (neutral incoherence), or CC3 (primitive replacement), rather than merely redescribing a centered theory.

## 1. Executive result

No surveyed route currently yields an unconditional derivation:

\[
CompleteActuality\Rightarrow AtLeastOneIrreducibleOpening.
\]

The strongest positive resources divide into four types:

1. **Nagai — constitutive centered actuality**: actuality is itself an opening, so ALO follows inside the centered actuality concept.
2. **Hare / Conitzer — presence-simpliciter conditional**: if monadic experiential presence is admitted, a distinguished I–NOW stage is strongly motivated.
3. **Michel Henry — self-manifestation transcendental condition**: appearing requires self-appearing / auto-affection, yielding at least manifestation, but not a unique or globally irreducible first-person opening.
4. **Psychophysical / nomological routes**: laws can determine subjects or determinate experiences, but unless the law's target semantics already include an absolute opening, they do not establish that some opening exists simpliciter.

So the current ALO frontier is not “which mechanism selects an opening?” It is:

\[
\boxed{
What independently forces actuality to be a centered kind rather than a neutral kind?
}
\]

## 2. Nagai: strongest constitutive existence route

Primary repository note: `literature/nagai-2007-2010-opening-actuality.md`.

Nagai's *The Opening* series explicitly links:

\[
WorldActuality=I!=NOW=Opening.
\]

On this picture, actuality is not first given neutrally and then assigned a center. Complete actuality obtains only as opened here-now.

This yields a clean existence conditional:

\[
Actual(R)+OIP\Rightarrow\exists\sigma\;Opening(R,\sigma).
\]

That is the closest existing literature to ALO.

But it does not yet satisfy CC2 against Neutral Actuality. Nagai gives a mature metaphysics of intrinsically centered actuality; he does not demonstrate a contradiction in uncentered actuality. His own appeal to sheer contingency / God's will at the actualization stage makes clear that the centered structure is constitutive or primitive rather than derived from neutral lower facts.

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{Nagai\ gives\ ALO\ inside\ C_C,\ not\ C_N\Rightarrow C_C.}
\]

Its strongest role is CC3: a serious primitive-kind competitor for neutral actuality.

## 3. Hare: monadic experiential presence as an apparent witness

Caspar Hare's egocentric presentism / perspectival realism treats the experiences of a privileged subject as **present**, not merely present-to-that-subject. The public descriptions of *On Myself, and Other, Less Important Subjects* characterize the core move as replacing:

\[
PresentTo(e,S)
\]

with:

\[
Present(e).
\]

If this phenomenological/metaphysical datum is independently accepted, it is already existential:

\[
\exists e\;PresentSimpliciter(e).
\]

Since an experiential event has a bearer and a time, Hare-style presence can support:

\[
\exists\langle S,t\rangle\;Distinguished(S,t).
\]

The difficulty is independence. A neutral theorist can reinterpret the same local evidence as:

\[
PresentTo(e,S,t)
\]

plus ordinary for-me-ness, without admitting monadic presence. Thus the positive datum is contested at exactly the point needed for ALO.

So Hare does not yet provide a neutral CC1 witness; he provides a direct phenomenological motivation for the centered completion rule.

## 4. Conitzer: the best conditional personalization argument

Vincent Conitzer's *The Personalized A-Theory of Time and Perspective* argues that if one accepts A-theoretic presence simpliciter, the same motivation favors a distinguished I as well as a distinguished Now. In his presence discussion, a current experience's liveness is tied to both temporal and personal location, and he explicitly argues toward a metaphysically distinguished I–Now pair/person-stage.

This supports the repository's Personalized-Presence Principle:

\[
PresenceSimpliciter(E)
\Rightarrow
\exists S,t\,[Bearer(E,S)\land Occurs(E,t)\land Distinguished(S,t)].
\]

For ALO, this is stronger than a mere analogy because one experiential occurrence jointly bears the temporal and personal coordinates.

But Conitzer explicitly brackets whether the classic A-theory arguments defeat B-theory. Therefore the route remains conditional:

\[
\boxed{
AcceptPresenceSimpliciter
\Rightarrow
AtLeastOneDistinguishedI\text{-}NOWStage.
}
\]

It does not establish:

\[
NeutralActuality\Rightarrow PresenceSimpliciter.
\]

## 5. Michel Henry: self-manifestation gives manifestation, not ALO

Michel Henry's phenomenology of life offers a powerful transcendental claim: if appearing itself must appear, avoiding regress requires self-appearing / auto-affection rather than purely intentional manifestation. The current SEP treatment reconstructs Henry's view as grounding intentionality in pre-intentional self-revelation or life.

This can be abstracted as:

\[
PhenomenalAppearing\Rightarrow SelfManifestation.
\]

For the project this is useful because it resists a picture of consciousness as wholly externally describable presentation.

However, Henry's later architecture explicitly allows Absolute Life to manifest through many individual livings. Hence:

\[
OneOriginarySelfManifestationGround
\not\Rightarrow
OneIrreducibleOpeningLocus.
\]

Even for mere ALO, a neutral theorist can accept local self-manifestation at every conscious subject without adding a global simpliciter opening. Henry therefore strengthens the ordinary manifestation layer, but does not force the absolute layer.

So:

\[
\boxed{SelfManifestation\not\Rightarrow AbsoluteOpening.}
\]

## 6. Psychophysical laws: existence only if the target semantics are already centered

Existing psychophysical-law literature establishes several legitimate law forms:

\[
PhysicalState\to PhenomenalQuality,
\]

\[
PhysicalCandidates\to SubjectBearers,
\]

\[
CandidateMentalOutcomes\xrightarrow{chance}OneActualMentalOutcome.
\]

These demonstrate that mind/subject individuation and determinate experience can be law-governed.

But ALO needs:

\[
L_\Omega:\quad R\to\exists\sigma\;AbsoluteOpening(\sigma).
\]

The mere coherence of such a law does not independently motivate its consequent. If `AbsoluteOpening` is simply inserted into the law's primitive target vocabulary, ALO has been stipulated nomologically.

Thus NOM / SAOL are valuable **realization architectures** once centered actuality is accepted, but they do not yet establish why reality must contain any absolute opening.

## 7. Four existence routes and their exact status

### E1 — Constitutive actuality

\[
Actuality=Opening.
\]

Best representative: Nagai / role-first centered completion.

Status: coherent positive primitive-kind theory; not a derivation from neutral actuality.

### E2 — Presence witness

\[
PresenceSimpliciter(E)\Rightarrow Opening(E).
\]

Best representatives: Hare + Conitzer.

Status: strongest conditional bridge; neutral side disputes monadic presence.

### E3 — Manifestation necessity

\[
Appearing\Rightarrow SelfAppearing.
\]

Best representative: Henry.

Status: supports local/possibly universal manifestation; fails absolute-layer promotion.

### E4 — Nomological existence

\[
Law(R)\Rightarrow\exists Opening.
\]

Best representatives: psychophysical selection / NOM / SAOL analogues.

Status: legitimate law form, but existence semantics remain primitive unless independently motivated.

## 8. CC1 / CC2 / CC3 scorecard

### CC1 — independent completion witness

Hare-style monadic presence is the closest candidate, but the neutral relational reading remains live. Henry's self-manifestation is too local/generalizable. No new independent witness has been isolated.

Verdict:

\[
\boxed{CC1\ remains\ open\ but\ not\ advanced\ to\ theorem.}
\]

### CC2 — neutral actuality incoherence

None of Nagai, Hare, Conitzer, Henry, or psychophysical-law frameworks establishes a contradiction in an actuality that contains local consciousness and ordinary first-person structure but lacks global opening.

Verdict:

\[
\boxed{CC2\ still\ fails.}
\]

### CC3 — primitive replacement

Nagai is the strongest resource: centered actuality is a serious primitive-kind replacement rather than an add-on selector. Hare/Conitzer add phenomenological motivation for that primitive kind.

But neutral actuality remains independently intelligible enough that no replacement theorem follows.

Verdict:

\[
\boxed{CC3\ receives\ motivation,\ not\ decisive\ superiority.}
\]

## 9. New ALO funnel

The literature compresses Gate A into a much narrower fork:

\[
\boxed{
NeutralActuality
\quad|\quad
ConstitutivelyCenteredActuality
}
\]

with only two plausible non-circular routes toward the centered horn:

1. find an independently unavoidable monadic-presence / actuality datum;
2. show that uncentered obtaining cannot constitute actuality at all.

Everything else — self-manifestation, subject-selection laws, stochastic selection — currently starts too late.

## 10. Current verdict

\[
\boxed{
ALO\ is\ not\ derived\ from\ the\ current\ common\ core.
}
\]

The strongest positive formulation is:

\[
\boxed{
ActualityIsIntrinsicallyCentered
\Rightarrow
AtLeastOneOpening.
}
\]

This is a real existential solution inside Centered Completion, and Nagai shows it has mature philosophical precedent. But the decisive bridge still missing is:

\[
\boxed{
Why\ accept\ intrinsically\ centered\ actuality\ over\ neutral\ actuality?
}
\]

That question is exactly the existing Completion Fork, so ALO does not create a new independent gate beyond the latest actuality endgame; it identifies the existential content of the centered completion horn.
