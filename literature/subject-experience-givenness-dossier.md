# Subject–Experience Givenness Dossier (2026-10-08)

> Selective direct-source verification; not a systematic meta-review.  
> Research target: [C0-F Subject–Experience Identity Gate](../research/arguments/c0-f-subject-experience-identity-gate.md).

1. **Henry Taylor**, “The relation between subjects and their conscious experiences”, *Philosophical Studies* 177 (2020), 3493–3507 (online 2019). https://doi.org/10.1007/s11098-019-01379-w . **Open-access article sections 2–5 consulted.** Defends property-exemplification event identity: experience token as a subject instantiating a phenomenal property at a time. Addresses bearer necessity vs subject's ability to exist without that event; event individuation is a substantive theoretical choice. **Does not argue C0 global neutrality or that qualia are explained away.**

2. **Marie Guillot**, “I Me Mine: on a Confusion Concerning the Subjective Character of Experience”, *Review of Philosophy and Psychology* 8 (2017), 23–53. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13164-016-0313-4 , public fulltext https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5362670/ . **Abstract, discussion and conclusion consulted.** Differentiates for-me-ness, me-ness, mineness; argues the first doesn't entail the other two. Clinical thought-examples provide conceptual pressure and must not be sold as independently decisive evidence.

3. **SEP, Phenomenological Approaches to Self-Consciousness**, https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/self-consciousness-phenomenological/ . **Relevant intrinsic-self-givenness and ownership paragraphs consulted.** Reports proponents regard prereflective self-consciousness as intrinsic to first-order experience; this **does not by itself mean a separate metaphysical mode or global absolute subject**.

4. **Roberto Sá Pereira**, “For-me-ness: a critical assessment of the phenomenal mineness thesis”, *Cognitive Processing*, online 4 August 2026. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10339-026-01383-z . **Open-access introduction and conclusion consulted.** Argues that clinical/phenomenological arguments do not independently establish *universally primitive* experiential mineness. A contested interpretive case, not demonstration that no genuinely felt experiences occur.

5. **Luke Roelofs**, “The Unity of Consciousness, Within Subjects and Between Subjects”, *Philosophical Studies* 173 (2016), 3199–3221. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11098-016-0658-7 . **Publisher/ANU abstract verification only in current workflow.** Cross-subject co-consciousness/token sharing motivates questioning single-bearer identity; see [earlier literature record](roelofs-2016-between-subject-unity.md).

**Epistemic status:** PE-token example, E0–E4 and local/global inferences in the linked audit are analytical use of these views, not direct quotations or results proved by their authors.
