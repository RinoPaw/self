# Thick Phenomenal Property and Subjecthood — 2026-10-08 source dossier

> Companion to [Thick-q dilemma](../research/arguments/c0-f-thick-phenomenal-property-dilemma.md). Evidence levels must not be confused with proof of an ontological theory.

## 1. Nida-Rümelin 2018, full-text

Martine Nida-Rümelin, “The experience property frame work: a misleading paradigm”, *Synthese* **195** (2018), 3361–3387, DOI https://doi.org/10.1007/s11229-016-1121-1 ; online 2016. Source: [Springer open-access](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11229-016-1121-1).

**Read**: abstract, §§1–2, selected intervening arguments and conclusion. Qualia as attributes of a freestanding experienced event creates a misleading **experience-property framework**; instead the subject itself instantiates **experiential properties**. This account explains why a second perception of the experience-object is unnecessary; it does **not** noncircularly derive experiential nature from nonphenomenal properties. Nida-Rümelin's conclusion does not pronounce on an absolute global I–NOW.

## 2. O'Conaill 2024, full-text

Donnchadh O'Conaill, “Subjecthood transparency”, *Analysis* **84**(3) (2024), 504–511, DOI https://doi.org/10.1093/analys/anad086 . [Open access](https://academic.oup.com/analysis/article/84/3/504/7633898).

**Read**: §§1–5. O'Conaill objects to an inference from the transparency of phenomenal concepts to the complete essence of subjecthood. Present experience may be compatible with subjecthood that persists *without currently experiencing* (dreamless sleep/coma example); no metaphysical verdict about that example is claimed. A phenomenal property's conceptual transparency would not settle the subject's grounded capacity to experience. This blocks an alleged automatic revelation of a fundamental mode identity from direct conscious acquaintance.

## 3. Millière 2025, verified abstract

Raphaël Millière, “Constitutive Self-Consciousness”, *Australasian Journal of Philosophy* **103**(3) (2025), 617–637, DOI https://doi.org/10.1080/00048402.2024.2417750 . First online 2024-12-11. Publisher [abstract](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/00048402.2024.2417750) and [Macquarie University publication record](https://researchers.mq.edu.au/en/publications/constitutive-self-consciousness/).

**Abstract-level reading only**. Distinguishes deflationary vs inflationary claims that self-consciousness is a constitutive feature of all consciousness; argues that the former is plausible at most and that the latter lacks sufficient support. Do not attribute our mode/grounding analysis to the author; the analogy to C1 is our own.

## 4. Taylor 2020 and phenomenology, primary text

Henry Taylor, “The relation between subjects and their conscious experiences”, *Philosophical Studies* **177** (2020), 3493–3507, DOI https://doi.org/10.1007/s11098-019-01379-w . [Open-access full article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11098-019-01379-w), §§2–5.

The PE (property-exemplification) event model is a subject instantiating a genuine phenomenal property at a time; the subject is a constitutive token-identity element. Taylor explicitly cites Nida-Rümelin's work as a motivation for subject-borne phenomenal properties.

SEP [Phenomenological Approaches to Self-Consciousness](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/self-consciousness-phenomenological/), revision 2023-12-19: prereflective awareness conceived as an **intrinsic** dimension of lived experience, not automatically a separately added higher-order act. Does not imply a **distinct ontic mode of obtaining** in our technical vocabulary.

## 5. What this dossier does not establish

Even jointly, these sources do not prove:

- subjective character identical with a neutral categorical property;
- subject-borne thick q is the ultimately correct global metaphysical ontology;
- a separately obtaining first-person mode is necessary or unnecessary;
- shared token experience is physically realized; or
- one actual reality includes exactly one fundamental global I–NOW.

They supply **different rival accounts**, explicit ontological cost centers, and a way to adjudicate the adequacy of C0-F without assuming C1/C2.
