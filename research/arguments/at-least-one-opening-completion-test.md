# At-Least-One Opening — Completion Test

> Date: 2026-10-05
>
> Status: argument-level integration after ALO literature audit.

## 1. Target

Gate A was previously written:

\[
ALO:\quad CompleteActuality\Rightarrow\exists\sigma\;IrreducibleOpening(\sigma).
\]

The repository's latest actuality endgame forces a refinement: `CompleteActuality` is itself disputed. Neutral Completion and Centered Completion use different actuality concepts.

So ALO must not bootstrap centeredness from the word “complete”.

## 2. Completion-sensitive decomposition

Let:

\[
K
\]

be the common core: one actual world/history, genuine other minds, local phenomenal/first-person structure, and all independently required temporal/perspectival facts.

Neutral Completion:

\[
C_N:\quad K+A_N
\]

where `A_N` is neutral actuality/obtaining.

Centered Completion:

\[
C_C:\quad \Omega[K;\sigma^*]
\]

where actuality is intrinsically centered.

Then the existential status differs:

### Under Centered Completion

\[
\Omega[K;\sigma^*]
\Rightarrow
\exists\sigma\;Opening(\sigma).
\]

ALO is constitutive.

### Under Neutral Completion

\[
K+A_N
\not\Rightarrow
\exists\sigma\;Opening(\sigma)
\]

unless an additional bridge is supplied.

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{ALO\text{ is equivalent to the existential content of the Completion Fork, not an independent theorem already available from }K.}
\]

## 3. Non-bootstrapping constraint

By the Completeness Non-Bootstrapping Principle:

\[
Complete(R)\equiv R\text{ contains an opening}
\]

cannot be used to infer ALO unless there is independent support for that completion criterion.

Thus the valid positive routes are only:

### A — Independent witness

\[
Independent(X)
\land
X\Rightarrow\exists Opening.
\]

### B — Neutral incoherence

\[
K+A_N\vdash\bot.
\]

### C — Primitive replacement superiority

\[
CenteredActuality
\]

must replace neutral actuality with equal-or-lower theoretical cost while doing more common explanatory work.

These are exactly CC1 / CC2 / CC3.

## 4. Presence-simpliciter route

Hare/Conitzer give the strongest conditional existential bridge:

\[
PresenceSimpliciter(E)
\Rightarrow
\exists\langle S,t\rangle\;Distinguished(S,t).
\]

Call this **PEB — Presence Existence Bridge**.

PEB is genuinely existential: unlike singularity arguments, it starts with at least one monadically present experience.

But the neutral rival can redescribe the evidence as relational/local:

\[
PresentTo(E,S,t)
\]

without monadic presence.

Therefore PEB has the form:

\[
\boxed{AcceptMonadicPresence\Rightarrow AtLeastOneDistinguishedStage.}
\]

It does not yet yield:

\[
K\Rightarrow AcceptMonadicPresence.
\]

So PEB is a conditional bridge, not a neutral witness theorem.

## 5. Nagai route

Nagai's strongest thesis can be reconstructed:

### OIP — Opening Identity Principle

\[
WorldActuality=I!=NOW=Opening.
\]

Then:

\[
\exists R\;Actual(R)
\Rightarrow
\exists\sigma\;Opening(R,\sigma).
\]

This is the cleanest ALO solution available.

However the explanatory direction matters. OIP is a conception of actuality itself, not a result derived from a neutral actuality base.

Hence:

\[
\boxed{OIP\ solves\ existence\ constitutively,\ but\ does\ not\ defeat\ Neutral\ Completion.}
\]

Its main contribution is to show that Centered Completion can be primitive without using a later selector.

## 6. Self-manifestation route

Henry-style argument:

\[
Appearing\Rightarrow SelfAppearing.
\]

Even if granted, this yields manifestation at the consciousness/life layer.

It does not establish:

\[
SelfAppearing\Rightarrow IrreducibleOpeningSimpliciter.
\]

Multiple local self-manifestations remain possible, and Henry's Absolute Life can manifest through many living individuals.

Therefore record:

### MOG — Manifestation-to-Opening Gap

\[
\boxed{SelfManifestation\not\Rightarrow AbsoluteOpening.}
\]

This prevents future regress to “consciousness is self-manifesting, therefore there must be an absolute first person.”

## 7. Nomological route

Psychophysical law can legitimately have subject-sensitive output:

\[
PhysicalCandidates\to SubjectBearers,
\]

or determinate mental actuality:

\[
CandidateMentalOutcomes\to OneActualMentalOutcome.
\]

But a target law:

\[
L_\Omega(R)\Rightarrow\exists\sigma\;AbsoluteOpening(\sigma)
\]

only solves ALO if `L_\Omega` is independently required.

If its fundamental content already says “there is an absolute opening”, then the existence fact has been relocated into nomology.

Call this:

### NER — Nomological Existence Relocation

\[
\boxed{Law\text{-}governed\ existence\neq Derived\ existence\ unless\ the\ law\ is\ independently\ motivated.}
\]

NOM / SAOL remain useful realization models after centered actuality is accepted; they do not currently force ALO.

## 8. ALO funnel

All serious routes now collapse to:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{array}{ll}
A.& PresenceSimpliciter\to Opening;\\
B.& Actuality=Opening;\\
C.& NeutralActuality\ incoherent;\\
D.& IndependentlyRequiredLaw\to Opening.
\end{array}}
\]

Current status:

- A: conditional, disputed by relational presence;
- B: coherent and strongest positive model, but primitive-kind choice;
- C: no contradiction established;
- D: no independently required absolute-opening law found.

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{K\not\Rightarrow ALO\quad\text{on current evidence}.}
\]

## 9. Interaction with USL

USL remains valuable:

\[
OneWorld+NF+FPNC\Rightarrow AtMostOneOpening.
\]

If any ALO route succeeds:

\[
AtLeastOne+USL\Rightarrow ExactlyOne.
\]

This means research should not spend more effort on independent uniqueness mechanisms. The decisive positive burden is still existential/completion-theoretic.

## 10. Interaction with locality

PFIP / BDP / global-subject-maximality work has narrowed the Universal-I escape, but it activates only after at least one irreducible center is granted.

So the dependency graph is now:

\[
\boxed{
Completion/ALO
\to
USL\ (singularity)
\to
Locality\ stress\ test
}
\]

with locality not supplying evidence backward for ALO.

## 11. Strongest present verdict

The most defensible positive theorem is conditional:

\[
\boxed{
ConstitutivelyCenteredActuality
\Rightarrow
AtLeastOneIrreducibleOpening.
}
\]

Combined with unitary reality:

\[
\boxed{
CenteredActuality+OneWorld+NF+FPNC
\Rightarrow
ExactlyOneIrreducibleOpening.
}
\]

And with a locality principle strong enough to block Universal-I:

\[
\boxed{
CenteredActuality+USL+Locality
\Rightarrow
OneLocalIrreducibleOpening.
}
\]

The unsolved first premise is no longer hidden:

\[
\boxed{Why\ is\ actuality\ intrinsically\ centered?}
\]

That is the current deepest positive question.

## 12. Research consequence

Future ALO work is high-value only if it does at least one of:

1. turns monadic presence into an independently unavoidable datum rather than a centered interpretation of local phenomenology;
2. proves neutral actuality incoherent;
3. finds an independently required law/structure whose output necessarily has first-person arity;
4. establishes a genuine primitive replacement advantage for centered actuality on common explananda.

Further constructions that merely assume an opening and model its selection/dynamics do not advance Gate A.
