# Builes Eight Arguments — Closest-Foil Audit

> 状态：systematic positive-case audit。
>
> 目标：把 Builes 2024 的八项 First-Person Realism arguments 一项一项对当前最强 neutral one-world controls 做 closest-foil test，而不是拿粗糙 third-person objectivism 当唯一对手。

## 0. Test standard

当前 absolute thesis：

\[
A:\quad \exists!E^*\;\Omega[R;E^*].
\]

当前 strongest neutral controls 至少包括：

### N-W\(_{thin}^+\)

\[
Consciousness
+
SubjectInvolvement
+
EgocentricOrganization
+
DeSe/SelfModel
+
OneWorld
+
NonFragmentation
+
\neg\Omega.
\]

### N-W\(_{thick}^+\)

再允许：

\[
\forall i\;PhenomenalForMeNess(E_i,S_i),
\]

甚至 world-side relational/perspectival facts：

\[
@_{s_i}(p_i),
\]

但仍没有 global winner。

一项 Builes argument 若要给 absolute axis 独立支持，最好找到：

\[
\boxed{
Independent(X)
\land
N\text{-}W^+\not\models X
\land
(N\text{-}W^++\Omega)\models X.
}
\]

仅支持 ordinary FP / StrongFPR 不足以关闭 Bridge B。

---

## 1. Anti-Physicalism Analogy

### Builes pressure

third-person facts留下：

- first-person knowledge gap；
- first-person explanatory / vertiginous gap。

如果 consciousness epistemic gap支持 anti-physicalist metaphysics，first-person parallel gap也有相似 prima facie force。

### Against N-W

**Knowledge gap** 很容易被 N-W 吸收：

\[
ThirdPersonKnowledge
\not\Rightarrow
SelfLocation.
\]

Perry/Lewis-style de se structure正面解释这点，无需 \(\Omega\)。

**Explanatory gap** 更危险：

\[
Why\ am\ I\ this\ subject?
\]

但它当前卡在：

\[
\boxed{\text{Vertiginous Question–Fact Gap}.}
\]

N-W 拒绝 global winner fact，而不是尝试解释它。

### Verdict

- Bridge A support：**medium/strong**；
- Bridge B support：**unresolved, not established**；
- strongest surviving subargument：vertiginous residual。

---

## 2. A-Theory Analogy

### Builes pressure

若 Prior-style temporal attitudes支持：

\[
Present_{simpliciter},
\]

则 “thank goodness I’m David” 看起来平行支持 first-person perspective facts。

### Against N-W

N-W 可以：

1. reject A-theory；
2. accept A-theory but deny subject/time perfect analogy；
3. accept subject-relative/world-side perspectival facts without one absolute subject；
4. explain relief via identity/de se practical structure。

即使 temporal privilege成立：

\[
AbsoluteNOW
\]

也没有形式蕴涵：

\[
AbsoluteI.
\]

需要一个 I–NOW coupling principle。

### Verdict

- conditional symmetry motivation：**real**；
- independent absolute-I residual：**not isolated**；
- Bridge B：**open**。

---

## 3. Anti-Haecceitism

### Builes pressure

first-person Castor/Pollux cases似乎有两个 genuinely different possibilities：

\[
I\ live
\quad vs\quad
I\ die,
\]

即使 uncentered qualitative structure相同。

### Against N-W

centered modal space可表示：

\[
\langle W,A\rangle
\quad vs\quad
\langle W,B\rangle,
\]

而无需说其中一者是 reality-level absolute winner。

self-locating / centered possibilities足以解释 why first-person case更 compelling than third-person permutation。

### Verdict

- centered possibility / modal self-location support：**strong**；
- StrongFPR support：**possible**；
- \(\Omega\) support：**weak / no independent step**。

---

## 4. Personal Identity — Dissociation

### Builes pressure

fission 中：

\[
David\text{ may fail to survive}
\]

while first-personally one seems to conceive：

\[
I\to Red,
\quad
I\to Blue,
\quad
I\to none.
\]

这提示 “me” 可能 dissociate from any ordinary person/body/soul continuation fact。

### Against N-W

neutral theory可以使用：

- branching survival；
- successor-relative de se facts；
- centered future possibilities；
- pre-fission uncertainty / anticipation without one global actual successor。

如果 absolute theory坚持：

\[
\Omega_t\to\Omega_{t+1}^{Red}
\]

or Blue only，则它新增 branch-selection fact。

### Verdict

- genuine pressure on simple identity reduction：**strong**；
- unique absolute successor：**not forced**；
- absolute theory gains a new burden rather than free support。

---

## 5. Personal Identity — Binary / Determinate / Non-Conventional

### Builes pressure

“will I be tortured tomorrow?” 似乎要求 yes/no、determinate、non-conventional answer；continuity theories often generate degrees / cutoff arbitrariness。

### Against N-W

N-W 可以独立加入 ordinary persistence structure：

\[
Succ_i(E_t,E_{t+1})
\]

或 primitive token identity facts。

多个 subjects 都可以有 determinate persistence relations：

\[
\forall i\;DeterminateSucc_i.
\]

没有：

\[
\exists!i\;Absolute_i.
\]

所以：

\[
\boxed{PersistenceDeterminacy\not\Rightarrow AbsolutePrivilege.}
\]

此外 binary/determinacy intuition 本身也是 personal-identity debate中的 substantive premise。

### Verdict

- possible ordinary metaphysical residual：**yes**；
- specifically absolute residual：**no current reason**。

## 6. Structural Features of Consciousness

### Builes pressure A — subjectivity

consciousness essentially perspectival；fundamentally objective physics似乎不能解释 point of view。

### Closest foil

N-W\(_{thin}^+\) already has：

\[
OriginTracking+EgocentricRegistration+SubjectInvolvement.
\]

N-W\(_{thick}^+\) can further accept：

\[
PhenomenalMineness
\]

and even：

\[
@_s(p).
\]

所以 “reality must contain point-of-view structure” 不再区分 N-W 与 A-W。

### Builes pressure B — unity

mental states within one stream cohere; across streams sharply separate。FPR offers common-point-of-view relation。

### Closest foil

N-W 可以用：

- local integration / psychophysical binding；
- standpoint-relative unity relation；
- structural subject boundaries；
- origin-tracking + global workspace / other consciousness architecture。

而 Builes 自己承认 alternative accounts exist。

### Verdict

- strongest support：**deep local perspectival ontology**；
- global singleton：**no**。

---

## 7. Mild Egocentric Hedonism

### Builes pressure

如果：

\[
ShouldCareMore(OwnPain),
\]

而 metaphysically all pains perfectly on par，则 normative asymmetry seems ungrounded。

FPR can ground：

\[
MetaphysicalSpecialness(OwnPain)
\to
NormativeSpecialness(OwnPain).
\]

### Against N-W

至少存在 competing grounds：

- agent-relative reasons；
- identity-relative prudence；
- agency/control；
- self-concern as constitutive practical standpoint；
- special normative relation without absolute metaphysical privilege。

所以正方需要证明：

\[
NormativeAsymmetry
\not\text{ adequately grounded by these alternatives}.
\]

再证明：

\[
\Omega\Rightarrow NormativeAsymmetry.
\]

第二条也不是自动的：如果 nonabsolute others genuinely suffer，为什么 \(\Omega\) 会降低/改变 their moral or prudential weight？

### Verdict

这是八项里除 vertiginous gap 外**最有希望成为 independent residual 的 candidate**，因为它诉诸 normativity 而非仅 redescribing first-person cognition。

但当前 bridge 尚未建立。

## 8. Symmetry Defense

### Builes pressure

arbitrariness objection同样打击：

- actual world privilege；
- present-time privilege。

不能只对 perspective privilege特别苛刻，除非有 relevant asymmetry。

### Closest-foil verdict

这是 defensive：

\[
\boxed{
\text{arbitrariness objection is non-unique}
\not\Rightarrow
\Omega\text{ exists}.}
\]

N-W 可以对 modal/time/perspective三者都采取 non-privilege / relative account；不存在 inconsistency。

如果 absolute theory接受 brute centered contingency，这个 defense可以减轻“brute = incoherent”的压力，但不会提供 positive evidence。

### Verdict

- coherence defense：**useful**；
- explanatory/source support：**none**。

## 10. Strongest-survivor principle

这轮审计后的正确做法不是说：

\[
\boxed{\text{Builes's arguments fail}.}
\]

它们成功显示 ordinary first-person domain远比粗糙 objectivism难处理。

真正结果是 target correction：

\[
\boxed{
\text{most of the arguments motivate centeredness / StrongFPR / perspectival structure before they motivate absolute singleton}.}
\]

对本项目最值得继续追的 survivors只剩两类：

### S1 — Vertiginous residual

\[
Why\ this\ center?
\]

必须通过：

\[
Question
\to
IndependentGlobalContrast.
\]

### S2 — Normative residual

\[
Why\ should\ own\ future\ welfare\ have\ special\ practical\ weight?
\]

必须证明 ordinary agent-relative / identity-relative normative resources不足，并建立：

\[
AbsoluteOrientation\to NormativePriority.
\]

其余六类目前主要推进 Bridge A 或 ordinary-centered metaphysics。

## 11. Current positive-case result

给 current closest foil 所有以下资源：

\[
N\text{-}W^+
=
\text{genuine consciousness}
+
\text{local subjectivity}
+
\text{de se}
+
\text{centered modal representation}
+
\text{optional thick mineness}
+
\text{optional world-side relational/perspectival facts}
+
\text{one coherent world},
\]

Builes 八项 survey 中目前没有一项已经建立：

\[
\boxed{
Independent(X)
\land
N\text{-}W^+\not\models X
\land
(N\text{-}W^++\Omega)\models X.
}
\]

所以当前可以记录一个**研究状态**而非 theorem：

\[
\boxed{
\text{the canonical positive FPR survey has not yet yielded an independent absolute-orientation delta against N-W+}.}
\]

这显著加强 `Absolute-Layer Surplus Pressure`，但没有证明 \(\neg\Omega\)。
