# Presence Singularity Principle Audit

> 状态：2026-10-05 PSP stress test。
>
> 目标：检验 `PresenceSimpliciter -> ExactlyOneAbsolutePresence` 是否有独立支持。

## 0. PSP

此前从 Personalized-Presence Bridge 得到：

\[
PresentSimpliciter(E)
\Rightarrow
\text{pressure toward a distinguished I–NOW bearer}.
\]

若再加：

\[
\boxed{
PSP:\quad
\exists E\;PresentSimpliciter(E)
\Rightarrow
\exists!E^*\;PresentSimpliciter(E^*)
}
\]

则可得到 unique absolute I–NOW person-stage。

本审计专测 PSP。

---

## 1. Effingham 2026: many presents are a live tense-theoretic option

Nikk Effingham 2026 的 `Now, Again and Again: The Metaphysics of Many Presents` 直接讨论：

\[
\boxed{\text{Can there be multiple present moments?}}
\]

并系统挑战几类常见反对：

- tense exclusivity；
- change；
- tense logic / semantics；
- open future。

这意味着：

\[
\boxed{
TenseRealism
\not\Rightarrow
ExactlyOnePresent.
}
\]

至少在 theory space 上，multiple-presents 不是明显 contradiction。

因此，即使 `present simpliciter` 是 genuine metaphysical status，也不能直接推出 singularity。

---

## 2. Fragmentalism / standpoint pluralism gives a stronger counterspace

Lipman 2026 的 standpoint pluralism / fragmentalism允许：

- temporal facts genuine；
- subjective facts genuine；
- multiple standpoints equally real；
- no time or subject ultimately privileged。

所以：

\[
\boxed{
PerspectivalFactRealism
\not\Rightarrow
OnePrivilegedPerspective.
}
\]

更强地说，现实可以包含多个事实栈 / standpoint-fragments，而无需一个 single God's-eye privileged stack。

这对 PSP 构成 general counterpressure：absolute-looking or standpoint-internal facts 可以 pluralize。

---

## 3. Solomyak-style pluralism widens the fork

Recent perspectival-pluralist literature treats modal, temporal, and first-person domains in parallel and explicitly explores views on which multiple perspectives have equal standing while perspectival facts remain real。

So:

\[
\boxed{
RealPerspectivality
\not\Rightarrow
MonismOfPrivilege.
}
\]

This mirrors the project's earlier Relative First-Person Demotion / fragmentalism results。

---

## 4. PSP verdict

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{
PSP\text{ is not a general entailment}.}
\]

The positive chain must now be written:

\[
PresenceSimpliciter
\xrightarrow{Conitzer/Hare}
Distinguished\ I\text{-}NOW\ carrier(s)
\xrightarrow{PSM?}
ExactlyOneAbsoluteOpening.
\]

where PSM is a new **Presence Singularity / Monism Principle**:

\[
\boxed{
PSM:\quad
\text{all absolute-presence facts belong to one ultimate opening}.}
\]

This is stronger and more honest than old PSP.

---

## 5. What could motivate PSM?

Candidate routes:

1. **single-stack realism** — complete reality must form one coherent unrivalled fact stack；
2. **one actual totality** — multiple absolute openings would amount to multiple actualities/worlds；
3. **non-compossibility** — two simpliciter-present I–NOW openings cannot jointly obtain in one complete actuality；
4. **Nagai-style non-pluralizability** — actuality/opening is essentially one；
5. **nomological one-hot law** — one opening is law-governed rather than conceptually forced。

None is currently established。

---

## 6. Main new burden

The core question is no longer:

> why does absolute presence have a subject/time coordinate?

Conitzer largely answers that conditionally。

The new question is:

\[
\boxed{
Why\ can\ there\ not\ be\ several\ equally\ absolute\ I\text{-}NOW\ openings?
}
\]

This is a pure singularity/monism problem。

---

## 7. Current verdict

- Presence-simpliciter route remains live against A-theoretic rivals；
- I–NOW coupling has serious support；
- **uniqueness does not follow**；
- Effingham 2026 and standpoint pluralism make plural absolute-presence structures a serious alternative。

Thus:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Presence semantics survives; Presence Singularity does not yet.}
}
\]
