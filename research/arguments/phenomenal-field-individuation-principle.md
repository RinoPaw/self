# Phenomenal-Field Individuation Principle — LUP Reconstruction

> Date: 2026-10-05
>
> Status: argument-level reconstruction after LUP literature audit.
>
> Goal: replace an over-strong unconditional `SameSubject -> PhenomenalUnity` premise with the strongest currently supportable conditional locality principle.

## 1. Why the old LUP is too strong

The previous locality route used:

\[
LUP_{old}:\quad SameSubject(e_i,e_j,t)\Rightarrow PhenomenallyUnified(e_i,e_j,t).
\]

That formulation suggests a constitutive metaphysical truth about numerical subject identity.

The literature does not currently justify that strength.

In particular, Bayne's Unity Thesis is best treated as a strong thesis about the organization of creature consciousness, with its necessity often characterized in biological/design-specification terms. Split-brain cases remain contested, and extra-phenomenal theories of subject identity can in principle hold one bearer fixed across disconnected experiential streams.

So:

\[
\boxed{BayneUT\not\Rightarrow ConstitutiveSubjectIdentityCriterion.}
\]

## 2. The stronger usable core

There is, however, a defensible conditional principle.

### PFIP — Phenomenal-Field Individuation Principle

\[
\boxed{
PhenomenalIndividuation(S)
+ MaximalDisconnection(F_1,F_2)
\Rightarrow
S(F_1)\neq S(F_2)
}
\]

where `PhenomenalIndividuation(S)` means that numerical subjects of experience are constituted or individuated by maximal structures of phenomenal organization rather than by an independently specified organism, primitive soul, or brute bearer.

`MaximalDisconnection(F_1,F_2)` means that no phenomenal-unity / co-consciousness / field-level connective structure makes `F_1` and `F_2` parts of one larger experiential whole.

PFIP is not a definition of all possible subjects. It is a conditional identity principle for phenomenal-organization-first theories of subjects.

## 3. Why PFIP has genuine support

### 3.1 Schechter-style split-brain pressure

If two simultaneous experiential systems support independent subjective perspectives and lack cross-field phenomenal unity, treating them as two subjects of experience is independently motivated even if they remain one person or organism.

This supports:

\[
IndependentPerspectives+PhenomenalDisconnection
\to
SubjectPluralityPressure.
\]

### 3.2 Bundle / thin-subject conceptions

On thin conceptions where subjects are maximal bundles of phenomenally unified experiences, PFIP follows directly from the individuation base.

Recent work also shows that the resulting structure need not be a neat integer partition: partial or intransitive unity may yield overlapping, vague, or intermediate subject structure. This refines PFIP rather than defeating its core.

The right claim is therefore not:

\[
Disunity\Rightarrow ExactlyTwoSubjects,
\]

but:

\[
\boxed{
SubjectStructureTracksMaximalPhenomenalOrganization.
}
\]

### 3.3 Anti-overlap pressure

Mørch's public argument against overlapping consciousness strengthens the non-overlap side of PFIP under a deflationary bundle view and assigns a parsimony cost to overlap under substantive-subject views.

This matters because a Universal-I spanning local subjects often requires one of two architectures:

1. local fields are parts of one globally unified field;
2. one global subject overlaps with or owns several locally unified subjects despite absent global unity.

PFIP directly pressures (2) on phenomenal-individuation views.

## 4. What PFIP does not prove

PFIP does not establish:

\[
\forall S\;PhenomenalIndividuation(S).
\]

A universalist can instead posit:

\[
PrimitiveBearer(I_U)
\]

and claim that numerically one subject bears many mutually disconnected experiential fields.

That model is coherent enough to block a theorem unless further argument is given.

So the live fork is:

\[
\boxed{
PhenomenalConstitutionOfSubjecthood
\quad|\quad
ExtraPhenomenalBearerIdentity.
}
\]

## 5. Universal-I under PFIP

The Universal-I now divides into two forms.

### U-PH — Phenomenally constituted Universal-I

\[
\exists!I_U\;\forall e\;ExperienceOf(e,I_U)
\]

with `I_U` individuated by phenomenal organization.

Then PFIP requires a global connective structure:

\[
\forall F_i,F_j\subset I_U,\quad ConnectedPhenomenally(F_i,F_j).
\]

If ordinary local fields are genuinely maximally disconnected, U-PH fails.

### U-PR — Primitive / extra-phenomenal Universal-I

One bearer owns all fields despite no global phenomenal unity.

PFIP does not refute U-PR. But U-PR now owes a new bridge:

\[
\boxed{
Why\ do\ disconnected\ fields\ belong\ to\ one\ bearer?
}
\]

Without an answer, “one universal subject” risks functioning as a global ownership label rather than an explanatory identity relation.

## 6. Roelofs and Osborn prevent a cheap locality argument

Roelofs keeps between-subject phenomenal unity and experience-sharing conceptually live. Therefore anatomical or organismic separation does not guarantee phenomenal disconnection.

Osborn keeps composite subjects conceptually live on a thin subject conception. Therefore local subject plurality does not by itself rule out a higher-order subject.

Together they block two invalid shortcuts:

\[
DifferentOrganisms\Rightarrow DifferentUltimateSubjects
\]

and

\[
ManyLocalSubjects\Rightarrow NoCompositeSubject.
\]

The project must establish **actual maximal disconnection** at the relevant level, not merely local biological boundaries.

## 7. Revised locality theorem schema

The best current locality chain is:

### ALO

\[
FirstPersonActuality\Rightarrow\exists\sigma\;Opening(\sigma).
\]

### USL

\[
OneWorld+NF+FPNC\Rightarrow AtMostOneIrreducibleOpening.
\]

### PFIP

\[
PhenomenalIndividuation
+ MaximalFieldDisconnection
\Rightarrow NoPhenomenallyConstitutedUniversalSubject.
\]

Then:

\[
\boxed{
ALO+USL+PFIP
\Rightarrow
OneLocalIrreducibleOpening
}
\]

**only conditional on** phenomenal individuation and actual maximal disconnection.

This is weaker than the previous `ALO + USL + LUP` presentation but better calibrated to the literature.

## 8. New deepest question

The LUP audit therefore moves the frontier one level down:

\[
\boxed{
What\ constitutes\ numerical\ subject\ identity?
}
\]

Three live families should be compared directly:

1. **Phenomenal-bundle / field views** — subject identity tracks maximal phenomenal organization.
2. **Causal / organismic / functional bearer views** — subject identity can outrun phenomenal unity.
3. **Primitive-subject views** — bearer identity is fundamental and can in principle span disconnected fields.

The next high-value research move is to compare these families on shared explanatory targets: experience ownership, duplication/fission, split brains, overlap, and higher-order subject combination.

## 9. Current verdict

\[
\boxed{
LUP_{old}\text{ is too strong; PFIP is the current defensible replacement.}
}
\]

PFIP does not defeat Universal-I outright. It creates a sharp burden split:

\[
UniversalI\Rightarrow
\begin{cases}
GlobalPhenomenalConnectivity, & \text{if subjecthood is phenomenally constituted};\\
IndependentBearerIdentityBridge, & \text{if subjecthood outruns unity}.
\end{cases}
\]

That is a substantive advance because Universal-I can no longer move costlessly between “one phenomenal field” and “one brute owner of many fields.”
