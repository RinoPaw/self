# B1 Constitution Stress Test — Explanatory Debt Is Not Automatically a New Fact

> 2026-10-08; adversarial status. Companion to [three C0 constitution packages](../models/c0-phenomenal-constitution-packages.md), [B1 residue audit](b1-explanatory-residue-audit.md) and [C1 grounding contrast](../models/neutral-contrast-grounding-audit.md). No thesis is proven true or metaphysically possible.

## 1. Strongest version of the challenge

A weak argument says: C0 lists all local qualia but cannot explain them; therefore we need exactly one absolute first-person perspective. This transition contains **two unearned claims**:

- that C0 cannot explain local phenomenal constitution by *any* allowed neutral actuality package;
- that a singular global perspective explains **why all** genuine local experiences obtain.

The improved test asks about **a specific factual and grounding target** P(i), for all ordinary subjects i, while comparing I, F and Q rather than attacking a merely physical inventory.

## 2. Four distinct levels of missing explanation

| Level | What fails | What it would support if independently established |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coverage | C0 rejects or omits an actually obtaining phenomenal fact P | Reject that C0 version; richer C0 variants might still survive |
| Grounding | P exists, but the offered identity/ground/bridge is unjustified | More ontic mechanism or an honest primitive; does not show a new global fact |
| First-person mode | Any legitimate neutral B1 account fails to constitute fact-identity of P as first-personally obtaining | Could support **C1 or C2**, never singleton alone |
| Global selector | Reality contains a further, independently identified, nonindexical and unique absolute winner | Could discriminate **C2**, but an independent witness remains absent |

The distinction between metaphysical **grounding** and **mere covariance/supervenience** is substantive (SEP Physicalism §§2.4 and Grounding §§1–3). A physical description that fixes a phenomenal truth need not explain it.

## 3. Challenge each of the C0 packages at its strongest

### C0-I: identity

Ask exactly which brain/organismic token is numerically identical to which felt fact, and whether both subject-bearing and phenomenal quality remain intelligible under identity. Mary's knowledge case and the phenomenal-concept literature challenge but do not resolve this identity claim: "new presentation, old fact" is a disputed defense.

**Failure condition**: justified nonidentity of the *actual phenomenal fact* from **every** admissible neutral identity base. A mere inability to verbally deduce the feeling from N is not enough.

### C0-F: fundamental subject-bearing qualia

Accept P(i) as an underived real fact. The critic must show why local phenomenal facts **cannot be legitimate primitive roots** unless a further irreducible mode M(i) constitutes their obtaining. The critic may not place an extra primitive mode in the definition of "genuinely felt" and announce victory.

**Failure condition**: a substantive fact-identity or actuality principle rules out all subject-bearing, objective-but-qualitative phenomenal primitives as adequate. The proposed principle must remain coherent when many subjects have real experiences. Even if true, the first inference points toward first-person arity; it does not yield only one mode.

### C0-Q: deeper categorical ground

Demand a proposed Q and bridge L_Q whose combined content specifically accounts for P(i)'s felt quality and bounded ownership, including multiple subjects. Pointing only to unspecified intrinsic quiddities leaves explanatory debt. Russellian neutral-monist approaches themselves face composition/quality challenges.

**Failure condition**: evidence or metaphysical argument shows no admissible Q, local composition law and organismic structure can constitute genuine experiences; ignorance of Q's exact nature alone does not establish universal impossibility.

## 4. Symmetric replacement test for C1 and C2

A C1 claim that M(i) is constitutive must show what *failed* in I/F/Q, which independent fact-identity/grounding constraint M(i) repairs, and how multiple M(i) coexist without contradictory objective facts.

A C2 claim that Omega* is constitutive must do that **and** explain why a globally unique role is indispensable. It must pay all still-unexplained local P(i) debts.

Concrete counterpressure: Suppose there are subjects a and b with independent genuine pain-experiences P(a) and P(b). Adding a privileged Omega(a) does not produce an explanatory grounding arrow for P(b) unless an explicit law connects Omega to b's local fact. Even a law that Omega actualizes *the entire world* has to show how it constitutes the felt quality of P(a) and P(b), rather than merely restating world actuality.

A global singleton may in principle replace many phenomenal roots with a more unified basis. **That would be a genuine positive result** only if the law grounding all P(i) can be independently specified and compared fairly with C0's neutral universal laws. Rebranding all ordinary phenomenal facts as Omega-manifestations without a constitutive mechanism is not yet an explanatory gain.

## 5. Brute-fact parity

Fundamental facts are allowed in many foundationalist programs, and any explanation may stop somewhere. C0-F openly stops at many phenomenal P(i); C2 may stop at one global role Omega*; C0-Q may stop at Q plus its bridge; C0-I may stop at a posteriori identities.

Do not count **number of primitive labels** as a complexity measure. A single universal law can be incredibly substantive; multiple ordinary qualitative facts can be cheap if a theory naturally generates them. Compare independent assumptions, explanatory closure, modal flexibility, consistency and empirical/phenomenological fit.

**Criterion**: if C2 retains each unexplained P(i) and simply adds Omega*, the absolute thesis has *not* reduced the relevant grounding debt. It may still have non-explanatory metaphysical appeal, but that is a separate dimension.

## 6. A reusable five-part ledger

For any candidate new witness X record:

1. **Fact**: formulate X in pre-C2 terms; show it actually obtains (not just that someone wonders about it).
2. **Base**: specify the strongest relevant C0-I/F/Q and full B1 (including local phenomenality).
3. **Failure**: identify exact identity, factivity, grounding or nomic constraint violated by that base.
4. **Repair**: spell out how C1 or C2 repairs **this same failed constraint**, not a new one.
5. **Net**: compare remaining primitive costs, especially grounding *other subjects'* P(i), and explain why C1 cannot do equally well if claiming C2 evidence.

Existing "why is it me?" intuitions, Mary, de se confusion, global actual world and Conitzer's external-display selector are relevant motivations/examples, but none has yet passed all five checks as C2-exclusive X.

## Verdict

The most robust immediate conclusion is **a debt-allocation result**: C0's admission of all local qualia does **not** entail adequate constitution, but C2's one absolute opening does **not** automatically improve the local consciousness grounding. The next positive research task is a substantive argument against C0-F's fact-identity notion, because it is the C0 option most willing to concede all local genuine experience while retaining subject-neutral global actuality.
