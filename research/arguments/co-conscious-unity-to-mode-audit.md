# U3: From Co-Conscious Unity to One Absolute Mode

> 2026-10-08；conditional theorem + countermodels. Does not establish C2 or refute C1/C0.  
> Earlier: [global unity audit](global-experiential-unity-audit.md), [GCR audit](gcr-unity-principle-audit.md).

## The missing bridge is now explicit

A claim that all experience forms one phenomenal unity decomposes into:

1. **Cover:** all actual episodes participate in the same co-conscious field.
2. **MI (Mode Identity):** co-conscious episodes have the same *fundamental first-person obtaining mode*.
3. **Reflection:** sameness of mode makes the actuality-constituting first-person facts jointly true at a single centered evaluation point.
4. **Localization:** if C2 further requires *this specific local I–NOW*, a unique global mode identifies one such token.

Each is stronger than the last in a different respect. The fourth need not follow from the third; a global field need not already privilege one ordinary person-stage.

Let E be nonempty actual episodes, C(x,y) co-consciousness, and mode(x) their putative ontic mode. **Cover + MI + realization of every claimed mode by some actual episode** entails exactly one realized mode:

\[
(\forall x,y\in E\,C(x,y))
\land(\forall x,y\in E\,[C(x,y)\to mode(x)=mode(y)])
\Rightarrow |\{mode(x):x\in E\}|=1.
\]

Proof: choose an arbitrary episode e. By Cover every x is co-conscious with e; by MI every mode(x)=mode(e). Nonemptiness gives at least one.

This **does not** prove GCR: sharing an ontic mode is not by itself a theorem about centered-truth supports. A further truthmaking/reflection axiom is required. Nor does it automatically give a privileged local stage.

## Three independent stress tests

**L1: Causal unity is weaker than phenomenal unity.** Two subjects exchange information and participate in one connected causal graph; their experiences can still be co-conscious only within each subject. Thus connectivity or one history does not establish Cover. Transitivity of co-consciousness cannot create a missing cross-subject co-conscious link.

**L2: Global co-consciousness does not formally entail MI.** Interpret two episodes a,b with C(a,b), but stipulate mode(a) different from mode(b), and supports {a}, {b}. This is consistent in a first-order signature *without MI*. Its metaphysical admissibility is disputed: if strong co-consciousness *means* numerically same subject/mode, the model is ruled out by that additional thesis. It only exposes the work performed by MI; it does not prove actual co-conscious episodes admit different ontic modes.

**L3: Unique phenomenal field does not immediately localize absolute I–NOW.** One global subject or experiential field could be a unified whole with distinguishable constituents. Moving from one field to a uniquely privileged *local* I-stage needs a further independent localization principle.

A promising U3 defense must therefore motivate global cross-subject bonding (Cover), explain mode individuation (MI) without defining it to favor C2, and establish mode-to-centered truthmaking (Reflection). The old global-subject and universal-consciousness arguments are relevant but cannot be counted three times as separate evidence for all three arrows.

## Source calibration

The SEP *The Unity of Consciousness* (revised 21 April 2025) describes subject-relative unity (including Bayne's Unity Thesis), Dainton's co-consciousness proposals, and the live dispute over split-brain streams. It gives no universal cross-subject Cover proof: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/consciousness-unity/ .

The SEP panpsychism supplement discusses subject-summing and the cosmopsychist decombination problem; a cosmic subject does not remove all grounding burdens: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/panpsychism/supplement.html .

List, *A quadrilemma for theories of consciousness*, *Philosophical Quarterly* 75(3), 2025, explains why non-fragmentation is a substantive choice within a larger competing theory space: https://doi.org/10.1093/pq/pqae053 .

**Verdict:** LocalUnity does not imply Cover. Cover implies one ontic mode only *conditional on MI* (and realization assumptions); one mode implies GCR / localized C2 only with further bridges. This is a positive conditional route with visible, independently contestable costs.
