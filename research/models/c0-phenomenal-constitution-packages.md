# C0 Consciousness Constitution — Three Competing Packages

> 2026-10-08 | Status: constructed candidate grounding accounts; **none has been proven metaphysically adequate**. Builds on [B1 residue audit](../arguments/b1-explanatory-residue-audit.md) and [Neutral Actuality Core](neutral-actuality-core.md).

## Common explanandum

A single actual history R contains conscious subjects a,b and real local phenomenal facts P(a,q,t), P(b,r,t); both have genuine felt experience, subject-relative mineness, ordinary de se, interpersonal relations and memories. The point is to explain why each phenomenal fact *obtains* while neither assuming a further global privileged center nor deleting anyone's consciousness.

Distinguish: **coverage** (P is included), **identity** (P is numerically the very same fact as an underlying fact N), **grounding** (P obtains *in virtue of* N), and **access** (a subject acquires de se/phenomenal knowledge). Supervenience and truth-functional determination do not settle grounding direction.

C0 means **subject-neutral actuality**, not necessarily reductive physicalism. Genuine fundamental phenomenal facts can exist in one world without an additional irreducible global first-person opening; the philosophical viability of this distinction is disputed.

## Package I: A posteriori phenomenal identity

Proposal: P(i,t) and a specified realized organismic/neural fact N(i,t) are ontically identical despite different scientific and phenomenal modes of presentation. Ordinary de se is fixed by subject-relative cognitive states K(i,t); world actuality is independently neutral.

- **Positive claim**: if the identity I(P,N) is true, no separate phenomenal substance or global opening is needed just to add P to the inventory.
- **Unpaid debt**: identity I is a substantive conjecture. Why N has the felt quality, and whether a phenomenal concept/acquaintance distinction neutralizes Mary's gap, remain contested. The identity symbol alone proves nothing.
- **C1 objection**: first-person *obtaining manner* cannot be identical to a third-person N. Needs independent fact-identity/grounding evidence.
- **C2 objection**: even if all P are identical to N, actuality requires a privileged global subject. That additional requirement must be argued independently.

## Package F: Fundamental local phenomenality

Proposal: each genuine local P(i,t) is a fundamental **world-side** qualitative fact, with local subject-bearer information. A single world obtains in a neutral actuality package. No extra fact makes one subject more actual than the others.

- **Positive claim**: preserves all actual qualia without pretending that merely neural/behavioral description is felt experience.
- **Unpaid debt**: fundamental P is an explanatory stopping point, not a deeper derivation. Count the potentially numerous phenomenal roots, laws of psycho-physical coordination, causal-exclusion worries and subject boundaries explicitly.
- **C1 objection**: facts P may inherently require a mode M(i) that cannot be represented as a neutral subject-bearing fact; merely naming such a mode is insufficient to prove it.
- **C2 objection**: plurality of primitive P needs a globally singular realization. Requires an independently defended global truthmaking/center principle.

**Key test:** having local fundamental subjective facts does not by itself require *exactly one* primitive global first-person mode. Conversely, if P's identity essentially includes fundamental ontic mode(s), this particular F package moves towards C1; classification depends on substantive identity commitments.

## Package Q: Neutral categorical constitution

Proposal: a Russellian or neutral-monist **subtype** posits intrinsic categorical grounds Q beneath physics' structural/dispositional descriptions. The local configuration Q(i) plus organismic structure O(i) and proposed constitution law L_Q grounds P(i). Q itself is specified without singular first-person privilege.

- **Positive claim**: attempts genuine constitution of local qualitative experience from deeper categorical roots rather than a brute P.
- **Unpaid debt**: what Q is; whether L_Q explains rather than restates phenomenality; subject individuation; composition of many categorical contributors into one bounded, felt experience.
- **C1 objection**: categorical quality may still fail to supply constitutive *for-someone obtaining*.
- **C2 objection**: if Q grounds all subject experiences symmetrically, where would one privileged local I-NOW come from?

**Taxonomy constraint:** Russellian monism has physicalist, phenomenal and proto-phenomenal variants. A Russellian ontology can belong to different C0/C1 families depending on ontic modes. "Neutral" in neutral monism (basic properties neither mental nor physical) differs from our "neutral actuality" (no further globally privileged first-person opening).

## Explanatory debt ledger

| Dimension | Identity I | Fundamental F | Categorical Q |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Truly felt local experiences | Via contested identity | Explicit primitives | Via contested bridge |
| Ground of phenomenal qualities | I asserted, not derived | P is root | Q+organization+L_Q |
| Local ownership | Subject-bearing N | Bearer-indexed P | Constitution/boundary law |
| Actual shared world | Neutral actuality | Neutral actuality | Neutral actuality |
| Unpaid debt | Identity and phenomenal concepts | Bruteness, integration and multiplicity | Categorical nature and composition |
| Unique absolute I-NOW | Not implied | Not implied | Not implied |

A C2 single absolute opening cannot automatically explain *every other subject's* P. Unless it provides an independently motivated universal grounding law for all P(i), it **adds** a global privilege claim while reusing the same unresolved local consciousness costs. C1 likewise must show its primitive modes **actually ground** what C0 fails to ground and remain globally coherent.

## Verdict

These are **three distinct research constructions**, not three metaphysical success stories. The F package is the hardest C0 foil for C2's explanatory-residue route because it gives C2 every genuine local phenomenal truth and pays its own phenomenal-primitives cost openly. It leaves open deeper explanation, but a deeper unexplained why-question alone cannot establish a *single absolute center*.

The productive next move is to show which exact C0 bridge or root is unacceptable and whether C1 or C2 provides a genuinely lower-cost replacement, with the same ordinary phenomenal explanandum held fixed.

- [SEP Physicalism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/physicalism/), updated 2026-09-16: identity, realization, grounding and fundamentality versions differ; supervenience does not automatically entail grounding.
- [SEP Metaphysical Grounding](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/grounding/), archived [Spring 2024](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/spr2024/entries/grounding/): explanatory/determination readings of grounding, fundamentality and theoretical costs remain debated.
- [SEP Neutral Monism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/neutral-monism/), revised 2023: neutrality criteria and the mind/physical grounding program are disputed.
- [SEP Russellian Monism](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/sum2024/entries/russellian-monism/): categorical/phenomenal/protophenomenal families must be distinguished.
- Daniel Stoljar, ["Physicalism and Phenomenal Concepts"](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.0268-1064.2005.00296.x), 2005: publisher abstract argues against versions of the phenomenal-concept strategy. Do not label that debate settled.

**Source strength**: cited SEP entries/sections and publisher abstract; three package definitions and comparative verdict are our own reconstruction, not direct doctrines attributed to those sources.
