# Non-Solipsistic Egocentric Opening (NSEO)

> 状态：2026-10-05 positive model / first-person privilege refinement。
>
> 目标：保留 Hare-style `present simpliciter` / perspectival privilege 的语义，同时拒绝经典 egocentric presentism 的 solipsistic implication；与 Conitzer local I–NOW、Merlo subjectivism、Nagai Opening 组合成一个 genuine-other-minds compatible model。

## 0. Core distinction

必须区分两个层级：

\[
Conscious(S_i)
\]

与：

\[
PresentSimpliciter(S_i,t).
\]

NSEO 保持：

\[
\boxed{\forall i\;Conscious(S_i)}
\]

同时规定：

\[
\boxed{\exists!\langle S^*,t^*\rangle\;PresentSimpliciter(S^*,t^*)}.
\]

因此 other minds 的 ordinary consciousness 不被取消；唯一性只作用于更强的 actuality/presence role。

---

## 1. Hare supplies privilege semantics

Hare 的 egocentric presentism / perspectival realism 给出一个关键 semantic template：

- ordinary relational claim：`experience e is present to subject s`；
- stronger monadic claim：`experience e is present simpliciter`。

其核心直觉是：某些 experiences 不只是 present-to-me，而是 simply present。

NSEO 保留后一种 monadic / intrinsic privilege reading，但拒绝由此推出：

\[
\forall j\neq *\;\neg Conscious(S_j).
\]

也拒绝把他人的 experiences 降格成纯 functional states。

所以 NSEO 是对 Hare 的有意改造，不反写成 Hare 原理论。

---

## 2. Merlo supplies non-parity without erasing others

Merlo-style subjectivism 说明一个重要逻辑空间：

\[
\boxed{\text{different subjects / mental states can all be real without being metaphysically on a par}.}
\]

这使以下组合保持 coherent-looking：

\[
Conscious(S_1)\land\cdots\land Conscious(S_n)
\]

以及：

\[
Privileged(S^*).
\]

NSEO 比 Merlo target 更强，因为它要求 simpliciter / absolute privilege，而不只是 subject-relative privilege；但 Merlo 证明“genuine others + asymmetrical metaphysical status”并不自相矛盾。

---

## 3. Conitzer supplies local I–NOW

Conitzer 的 personalized A-theory 允许 distinguished I 与 distinguished Now 合成一个 local centered unit：

\[
\boxed{I\text{-}NOW=\langle S^*,t^*\rangle.}
\]

该 unit 可局域于 spacetime 中一个 observer/person-stage，而无需 universe-wide absolute simultaneity。

因此 NSEO 默认不要求：

\[
GlobalPresentSlice(t^*).
\]

而只要求：

\[
\boxed{AbsolutePresentStage(S^*,t^*)}.
\]

这显著降低与 relativity 的直接冲突。

---

## 4. Nagai supplies actuality-opening identity

Nagai 的 strongest template 把：

\[
WorldActuality,
\quad I!,
\quad NOW
\]

理解成同一 Opening 的几个方面。

NSEO 因而将 local I–NOW privilege进一步解释为：

\[
\boxed{
Open(R,S^*,t^*)
}
\]

其 intended semantics：

> complete actuality is open / actual through this conscious person-stage.

这不是 ordinary self-location：其他 subjects 仍有 genuine local first-person organization。

---

## 5. Model schema

令 actual complete base 为 \(R\)，其中 local conscious subjects/person-stages集合为：

\[
C(R)=\{\langle S_i,t_j\rangle\}.
\]

定义：

\[
O_R(S,t).
\]

NSEO 要求：

### Genuine other minds

\[
\forall S_i\in Subjects(R),\quad Conscious(S_i).
\]

### Unique opening stage

\[
\boxed{
\exists!\langle S^*,t^*\rangle\;O_R(S^*,t^*).
}
\]

### Opening semantics

\[
O_R(S^*,t^*)
\Rightarrow
PresentSimpliciter(Experience(S^*,t^*)).
\]

### No consciousness erasure

\[
\forall \langle S_j,t_k\rangle\neq\langle S^*,t^*\rangle,
\quad
Conscious(S_j,t_k).
\]

但：

\[
\neg O_R(S_j,t_k).
\]

---

## 6. Why this differs from classical egocentric presentism

Classical Hare-style reading can be represented as a stronger package：

\[
PresentSimpliciter(E^*)
+
\text{others lack comparable present consciousness}.
\]

NSEO explicitly weakens the second conjunct。

So：

\[
\boxed{
\text{AbsolutePresence}\not\Rightarrow\text{OnlyConsciousMind}.
}
\]

This is exactly the two-tier structure required by the project：

\[
\text{many ordinary first-persons}
+
\text{one absolute opening}.
\]

---

## 7. Main virtue

NSEO gives the project a cleaner local target than global-slice models：

\[
\boxed{
\exists!\langle S^*,t^*\rangle\;AbsoluteOpening(S^*,t^*)
}
\]

rather than：

\[
\exists!t^*\;GlobalPresentSlice(t^*).
\]

This avoids making absolute first-person theory depend on a preferred simultaneity hypersurface。

---

## 8. Main cost: privilege remains primitive unless law/constitution added

NSEO itself only clarifies ontology/semantics。It does not derive：

\[
O_R(S^*,t^*).
\]

from neutral consciousness or structure。

Therefore one still needs one of：

1. constitutive Opening：`actuality just is opened through one I–NOW stage`；
2. nomological Opening：a law `Λ_O` yields exactly one opening stage；
3. stochastic/symmetry-breaking Opening：symmetric candidates + law-governed asymmetric realization。

Without such machinery，NSEO is a target ontology，not an explanatory theory。

---

## 9. Relation to UIWO

UIWO used：

\[
O(w,t,s).
\]

NSEO suggests a leaner default：

\[
\boxed{O(R,s,t)}
\]

where `R` is already the actual complete history/base，and `t` is a local person-stage time rather than a global NOW slice。

This removes two optional burdens：

- no need for modal world-coordinate if actuality of R is already fixed；
- no need for global simultaneity。

The strongest version can still be expanded to UIWO when modal/temporal privilege is independently motivated。

---

## 10. Current verdict

NSEO is currently the cleanest ontology matching the original project constraints：

\[
\boxed{
\text{genuine other minds}
+
\text{one privileged I–NOW person-stage}
+
\text{present/actual simpliciter semantics}.
}
\]

It is better than classical egocentric presentism for this project because it preserves ordinary consciousness of others；better than bare structural dominance because the privileged predicate already has first-person/presence semantics；and leaner than a global-NOW model because privilege can be localized to one person-stage。

But it does not yet answer：

\[
\boxed{Why\ does\ reality\ open\ through\ exactly\ this\ person\text{-}stage?}
\]

That remains the nomological/constitutive Opening problem。

## Literature anchors

- Caspar Hare, *On Myself, and Other, Less Important Subjects* (2009)；`Realism About Tense and Perspective` (2010) — monadic presence / perspectival privilege semantic template。
- Vincent Conitzer, `The Personalized A-Theory of Time and Perspective` (2020) — local I–NOW, no required global simultaneity。
- Giovanni Merlo, `Subjectivism and the Mental` (2016)；Merlo & Pravato, `Relativism, realism, and subjective facts` (2020/2021) — real viewpoints need not all be metaphysically on a par。
- Hitoshi Nagai, `The Opening: A Philosophy of Actuality` (2007–2010) — actuality/I/NOW opening identity template。
