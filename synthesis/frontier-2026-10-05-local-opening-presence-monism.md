# Frontier — Local Opening / Presence Monism

> 日期：2026-10-05
>
> 状态：NSEO / Personalized-Presence / Presence-Singularity audit 收口。

## 1. Core update

当前 absolute-first-person target 可显著精简为 local person-stage opening：

\[
\boxed{
\exists!\langle S^*,t^*\rangle\;Open(R,S^*,t^*)
}
\]

无需默认加入 universe-wide global NOW hypersurface。

---

## 2. NSEO — genuine other minds + one absolute opening

Non-Solipsistic Egocentric Opening 保持：

\[
\forall i\;Conscious(S_i)
\]

同时：

\[
\exists!\langle S^*,t^*\rangle\;Present/ActualSimpliciter(S^*,t^*).
\]

Hare 提供 monadic presence / perspectival privilege semantics；Merlo-style subjectivism说明 genuine others 与 metaphysical non-parity并不矛盾；Conitzer 提供 local I–NOW；Nagai 提供 actuality-opening identity template。

---

## 3. Personalized-Presence Bridge

Conitzer 2020 给当前最强 conditional bridge：

\[
\boxed{
PresenceSimpliciter(E)
\Rightarrow
\text{pressure toward a distinguished I–NOW person-stage}.}
\]

原因不是 bare analogy；present simpliciter 的 bearer 是一个 experience，而 experience属于一个 subject-at-a-time。

因此 accepting A-theoretic presence while keeping I completely unprivileged requires a relevant asymmetry explanation。

---

## 4. Presence-Simpliciter Fork

Skow-style B-theory route可直接拒绝：

\[
PresentSimpliciter(E)
\]

并以 relational availability / temporal perspective解释 current experience。

所以：

\[
\boxed{
RejectPresenceSimpliciter
\;|\;
AcceptPresenceSimpliciter+I\text{-}NOWPressure.}
\]

这使正方结果严格 conditional，而非 universal proof。

---

## 5. Presence Singularity fails as entailment

Effingham 2026 直接把 multiple presents发展成 serious tense-theoretic option；Lipman 2026 / fragmentalism允许多个 temporal/subjective standpoints都 real、无一个 ultimately special。

所以：

\[
\boxed{
PresenceSimpliciter
\not\Rightarrow
ExactlyOnePresence.}
\]

uniqueness 必须由独立 monism principle承担。

---

## 6. Single-Stack / Unitism pressure

Presence Monism 最自然的 deeper principle：

\[
SSR:\quad CompleteReality\text{ forms one coherent, unrivalled fact-totality}. 
\]

若不同 absolute openings包含 incompatible simpliciter facts，则：

\[
SSR+Incompatibility
\Rightarrow
AtMostOneOpening.
\]

但 fragmentalism precisely rejects SSR，允许 reality 分成多个 incompatible fragments。

Recent mapping of fragmentalism makes the cost explicit：reality no longer composes one coherent unitary whole；coherent unitism is one main boundary theory。

因此：

\[
\boxed{
UnitaryReality\text{ exerts abductive pressure toward Presence Monism}.}
\]

不是 theorem。

---

## 7. Current three-way theory space

### M1 — Opening Monism

\[
\exists!O^*.
\]

one coherent complete actuality；Nagai/NSEO/NOM direction。

### M2 — Standpoint / Fragment Pluralism

multiple real temporal/subjective fact-stacks；no unique ultimate opening。

### M3 — Deflationary relationalism

no `present/actual simpliciter` first-person fact at all；ordinary self-location only。

所以：

\[
\boxed{M1\;|\;M2\;|\;M3}
\]

现在是比旧 `neutral vs centered` 更精确的 local-presence endgame。

---

## 8. Strongest positive chain

当前 strongest positive route：

\[
\boxed{
PresenceSimpliciter
\xrightarrow{Hare/Conitzer}
I\text{-}NOW\ person\text{-}stage
\xrightarrow{UnitaryReality/OpeningMonism}
OneAbsoluteOpening.
}
\]

第一箭头有 substantial literature support；第二箭头只有 constitutive/abductive support。

如果再加入 Nomological Opening / spontaneous symmetry breaking：

\[
OneOpeningLaw
+\text{law-governed asymmetric realization}
\]

则可说明 exact symmetry 下为何仍实际有 one opening，但仍不独立证明为什么 reality 采用 monist law rather than pluralist/neutral ontology。

---

## 9. Current judgement

本阶段最重要的收缩：

1. **global NOW 不再是核心 burden**；local I–NOW足够；
2. `I` 与 `NOW` 的 coupling已有 serious conditional derivation；
3. 真正剩余 hard gap 是 **singularity / unitism**；
4. fragmentalism把这个 gap变成明确 metaphysical fork，而非简单 consistency objection。

因此当前最底问题是：

\[
\boxed{
Why\ is\ complete\ reality\ one\ coherent\ opening\ rather\ than\ many\ equally\ real\ standpoint\ fragments?
}
\]
