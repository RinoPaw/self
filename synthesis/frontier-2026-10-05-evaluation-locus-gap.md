# Frontier 2026-10-05 — Evaluation-Locus Cardinality Gap

> Supersedes `frontier-2026-10-05-plural-opening-model.md` on the deepest singularity bridge.

## 1. New cut

Plural Opening Manifold showed:

\[
OneActuality+PluralIrreducibleFirstPersonModes
\]

is coherent-looking if reality uses mode-preserving global coherence rather than List-style same-perspective compossibility.

The next question was:

\[
Why\ SPC?
\]

A deeper audit of List's formalization reveals another layer.

List represents a first-personally centred world as:

\[
\boxed{\langle\omega,\pi\rangle}
\]

with **one** locus of subjectivity \(\pi\).

First-person facts are represented by sets of such centered worlds. Different subjects' complete first-person proposition sets have empty intersection because no single \(\langle\omega,\pi\rangle\) realizes both complete perspectives.

This proof is valid inside that semantic architecture.

But the architecture itself contains a cardinality choice.

---

## 2. Evaluation-Locus Cardinality Gap

First-person irreducibility / perspective variance gives:

\[
\exists \pi_i,\pi_j\;[V(F,\pi_i)\neq V(F,\pi_j)].
\]

Therefore the evaluation of \(F\) must retain perspective sensitivity.

It does **not** by itself give:

\[
\boxed{
Every\ maximal\ actuality/evaluation\ state\ contains\ exactly\ one\ perspective.
}
\]

Call the latter:

### MEP — Monocentric Evaluation Principle

\[
\boxed{
MaximalFPEvaluationLocus\Rightarrow ExactlyOnePerspective.
}
\]

So:

\[
\boxed{
PerspectiveVariance\not\Rightarrow MEP.
}
\]

This is the new **Evaluation-Locus Cardinality Gap (ELCG)**.

---

## 3. Polycentric alternative

Instead of maximal states：

\[
\langle\omega,\pi\rangle,
\]

allow：

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak R=\langle\omega,\Pi,\mathcal V,A\rangle,
\qquad |\Pi|>1.
}
\]

Each \(\pi_i\) retains its irreducible first-person valuation / obtaining mode：

\[
\mathcal V_{\pi_i}.
\]

There is still one actuality status \(A\) and one shared third-person history \(\omega\).

Perspective variance remains real：

\[
V(F,\pi_a)=1,
\qquad
V(F,\pi_b)=0.
\]

But complete reality is polycentric rather than one centered point.

This is the semantic counterpart of Plural Opening Manifold.

---

## 4. New decomposition of SPC

SPC says：

\[
Compossible_{FP}(F,G)
\Rightarrow
\exists\pi\;[\pi\Vdash F\land\pi\Vdash G].
\]

Within centered-world semantics, this follows naturally because candidate joint truth loci are all monocentric points.

So the deeper chain is：

\[
\boxed{
MEP
+Pointwise/Intersection\ Compossibility
\Rightarrow SPC.
}
\]

Then：

\[
SPC+LE\Rightarrow FPNC,
\]

\[
FPNC+NF_{SPC}\Rightarrow AtMostOne,
\]

\[
AtMostOne+ALO\Rightarrow ExactlyOne.
\]

The old exact-one result is therefore decomposed all the way to a claim about the cardinality of the maximal first-person evaluation locus.

---

## 5. Important fairness point

This does **not** refute List.

List is fully entitled to use centered worlds \(\langle\omega,\pi\rangle\) to represent a subject's complete first-person perspective. His empty-intersection result follows.

The new project question is metaphysical, not a complaint about his mathematics：

> why should one such centered locus, rather than a polycentric family/manifold of loci, be the maximal unit of complete actuality?

List's many-worlds theory answers by treating different centered worlds as different first-personal realizers. Fragmentalism answers by letting multiple incompatible perspectival fact stacks belong to one reality. Plural Opening Manifold develops the latter direction as one actual polycentric structure.

---

## 6. Monocentricity cannot be inferred from non-invariance alone

The following inference is invalid without more premises：

\[
FirstPersonFacts\text{ vary with }\pi
\]

therefore：

\[
CompleteActuality\text{ has one }\pi.
\]

Variance determines **dependence on a mode/index**.

MEP determines **how many modes a maximal actual structure may contain**.

These are different structural questions.

So：

\[
\boxed{
FirstPersonIrreducibility
+PerspectiveVariance
\not\Rightarrow
MonocentricActuality.
}
\]

---

## 7. Current strongest C1 semantics

Plural First-Person Actuality can now be represented in two equivalent-looking ways：

### Mode formulation

\[
\mathcal P=\langle W,C,M,F,A\rangle.
\]

### Polycentric evaluation formulation

\[
\mathfrak R=\langle\omega,\Pi,\mathcal V,A\rangle.
\]

Both preserve：

- one actual totality；
- multiple irreducible first-person modes；
- local consistency；
- no globally privileged perspective。

The second formulation connects directly to List's centered-world semantics and makes the exact disagreement transparent：

\[
\boxed{monocentric\ locus\quad|\quad polycentric\ locus.}
\]

---

## 8. What would defend MEP

MEP needs an independent first-person-specific argument, such as：

### M1 — Truthmaker singularity

A genuine first-person fact's maximal truthmaker is necessarily one standpoint-totality.

### M2 — Polycentric third-personization theorem

Any structure containing multiple perspectives at the maximal level becomes merely a third-person collection of perspective facts and loses genuine first-person status.

### M3 — Actuality singularity theorem

Actuality itself can instantiate only one first-person mode at a time / per complete actuality index.

### M4 — Unity theorem

A polycentric maximal state cannot count as one metaphysically unified actuality for reasons independent of the target singularity.

Current project has no proof of M1–M4.

The singularity frontier is now：

\[
